Written 2026-11-22. Backlinks are the biggest factor left in ranking against much older sites. The rules from the growth plan apply: every message is approved by the owner first, no mass mailing, no bought links, no link swaps, no comment spam, no fake accounts or reviews.

## Where to ask, in order of likely payoff

1. **Settlement and newcomer organisations** that list practical resources for people arriving in Canada or applying to IRCC. Offer the IRCC mailing guide and the address formatter as a resource. Slow, but these links are trusted.
2. **University international student offices** and **expat associations** (Canadians abroad, UK and US groups in Canada). They answer "how do I mail documents home" all the time.
3. **Journalists and writers covering the 2027 $2 stamp.** One short, factual note with our sourced history page, offered as a reference and with no ask for coverage.
4. **Genuinely helpful answers** on forums and Q&A sites where the question is "how do I mail X without a printer". Answer fully first. Mention us only where it truly fits, once, and disclose that it is our site. No repeated posting.
5. **Business profiles that are free and legitimate:** Google Business Profile (already live), Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, the local Chamber of Commerce, and the Canadian and US business directories you actually qualify for. These give citations more than ranking power, and they are safe.
6. **Local and trade press** for the founder story (a Canadian-run mailing service that mails letters for people abroad). Offer an interview, not an ad.

## Do not do

- Do not buy links, join link exchanges, or use "guest post" farms.
- Do not name or compare competitors in any outreach.
- Do not fake or incentivise reviews (a review line tied to a reward is already ruled out).
- Do not mark up our own reviews as a rating on our own pages. Google does not show stars for reviews of your own business on your own site, and it risks a penalty.

## Working rhythm (small and steady)

- Send about 5 approved messages a week, not hundreds.
- Record each one (who, which page, date, reply) in the admin Follow-ups screen so nobody is contacted twice and unsubscribes are respected.
- Review in the admin Traffic tab, under "other sites", which links send visitors.
- Re-check every 4 weeks. Drop what gets no response and keep what does.
