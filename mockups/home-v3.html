    Handed to the post. We email you when it goes out.

The fine print, up front

Refunds
Cancel any time before your letter goes to the printer and you get your money back in full.
Reprints
If your letter doesn't arrive, we reprint it free within 30 days in Canada and 60 days elsewhere. If the mistake is ours, we reprint it no matter what.
What we won't mail
Threats, harassment and anything illegal. Orders like that are refunded and not printed.
What we don't offer
Tracking numbers, signatures or proof of mailing. For legal deadlines, use registered mail at a post office.
Your file
Used only to print your letter. Card payments go through Stripe and we never see your card number.
