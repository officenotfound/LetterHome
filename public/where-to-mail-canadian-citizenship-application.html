Where to mail a Canadian citizenship application

Citizenship grant and resumption applications are mailed to the Case Processing Centre in Sydney, Nova Scotia, a specific PO Box, not a street address. IRCC processes citizenship paperwork centrally through CPC-Sydney, and uses different boxes for different purposes, grants, proof of citizenship, and documents it has specifically requested from you.

Updated October 2026

Whether you're applying for citizenship as an adult, applying on behalf of a minor, or applying for resumption of citizenship you previously held, the application package is mailed to the same processing centre. Using the correct box matters, IRCC sorts incoming mail by destination box.

CAF-Citizenship

Canada

Source: canada.ca — Case Processing Centre: Sydney, Nova Scotia

Courier is different from regular mail: if you're using a courier rather than Canada Post, IRCC's Sydney intake uses a street address at the IRCC Digitization Centre, 3050 Wilson Ave, New Waterford, NS B1H 5V8, not the PO Boxes above. Confirm which applies to your method of sending before you ship anything.
