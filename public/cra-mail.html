Why the mailing date matters

The CRA treats the mailing date as the filing date for most submissions by mail. If your return is due April 30 and you mail it April 30, the mailing date confirms timely filing, even if it takes a few days to arrive.

If you're in a time zone ahead of Canada, or abroad without access to Canada Post, Letterhome ensures your documents hit the Canadian mail system in time. Place your order before 11 PM Eastern and we mail the next business day.

CRA tax centre addresses

The CRA has multiple tax centres across Canada. The correct address depends on your province of residence and the type of document. The full list is at canada.ca/cra, tax centres.

Common centres include Sudbury ON, Surrey BC, Winnipeg MB, and Jonquière QC. Always use the address listed for your specific situation rather than a general CRA address.

Common questions

Does the CRA accept PDFs printed by a third party?

Yes. The CRA processes printed and signed copies of forms submitted by mail. The form itself is what matters, who printed it is irrelevant as long as it is complete and signed where required. We print exactly what you upload, so you are responsible for ensuring it is correctly filled out.

What if my filing deadline is today?

If you order today, we mail tomorrow (next business day). The CRA uses the mailing date as the filing date, not the arrival date. So ordering today still gets you tomorrow's mailing date. For same-day mailing date you would need to find a Canada Post location yourself, we can't guarantee same-day mailing.

Can I send multiple documents in one order?

Yes, up to 5 files per order. Standard lettermail handles up to roughly 6 to 8 sheets of paper (30 grams). If your submission is larger, place a second order and include a note in your cover letter that it is a continuation package.
