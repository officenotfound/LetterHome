Fax

Fax cover sheet:
what to include.

The first page of a fax tells the person at the other end who it is for and how many pages to expect.

Send a fax →

A fax cover sheet lists who the fax is for, who it is from with a phone number or email, the date, the total number of pages including the cover, and a short note. It matters most when a fax goes to a shared machine, so it reaches the right person and they can tell if a page is missing.

How it works

  1. Say who it is for

    The person's name and department, and their fax number.

  2. Say who it is from

    Your name and a way to reach you, such as a phone number or email.

  3. Count the pages and add a note

    The total pages including the cover, and one or two lines about what is attached.

Count the cover page: write the total number of pages including the cover sheet, so the person can check that nothing is missing.

A template you can copy

FAX

To: Name, department
Fax: 613-555-0123
From: Your name
Phone or email: how to reach you
Date: 2026-11-27
Pages: 4 including this page

Note: Please find my completed form attached, for file 12345.

Do you need one?

Not always, but it helps whenever a fax goes to an office where many people share one machine. Many forms and offices also ask for a reference number, such as a file or claim number, on the first page.

Common questions

What goes on a fax cover sheet?

Who it is for and their fax number, who it is from and how to reach you, the date, the total pages including the cover, and a short note.

Is a fax cover sheet required?

Not usually, but many offices expect one, and it helps the fax reach the right person.

Does the cover sheet count as a page?

Yes. Include it in the page count you write on it.
