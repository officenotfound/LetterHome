Plenty of Aussies spend a year or two in Canada on a working holiday, from the lifts at Whistler to the kitchens in Banff. Calling mum is easy. A letter she can stick on the fridge is better. Here's how to send one home.

How it works

  1. Write it online

    Type it on our site, or attach a PDF. Photos from the trip print in colour if you pick colour.

  2. Pop in the address

    Street, suburb, state and the four-digit postcode, with AUSTRALIA on the last line.

  3. We print and post it

    A person checks the order, then it's printed, put in an envelope and posted. We email you when it goes.

What it costs

Letters to Australia are priced in Canadian dollars, so there's no foreign exchange fee from us. Where our Australian printing is running, a letter is printed and posted inside Australia. Otherwise it's printed in Canada and posted from there, and the price is the international one. The Australia page shows the current estimate in Australian dollars, and the order page shows the exact price before you pay.

Ideas for what to write

  • The first snowfall you ever saw, and how badly you handled it.
  • A photo of your Canadian flatmates, with names, so mum can stop asking.
  • A birthday letter that's timed to land on the day. Schedule it for later.
  • A thank-you letter to the people who helped you get over there.

Worth knowing

  • International post to Australia typically takes up to 4 weeks, so don't leave a birthday letter to the last minute.
  • Standard post has no tracking, so we can't give an exact arrival date.
  • For Christmas, order early. See our holiday order-by dates.

Common questions

Can I send a letter home to Australia from Canada?

Yes. Write it on our site and we print it and post it to any Australian address.

How long will it take?

International post typically takes up to 4 weeks. Where our Australian printing is running, it can be quicker, and the order page tells you before you pay.

Can I include photos?

Yes. Attach them and pick colour printing so they come out in colour.

Do I need an Australian stamp?

No. Postage is included in the price.
