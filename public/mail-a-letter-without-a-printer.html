How it works with Letterhome

  1. Enter the two addresses. The sender address becomes the return address on the envelope. You can skip it and send without one.
  2. Write the letter. Type it in the box, or attach a PDF or Word file. You see the exact printed page before you pay.
  3. Pay. We check the address, print the letter, put it in an envelope with postage and mail it within two business days.

The order form works on a phone, so a document that only lives on your phone is fine. There is no app to install and no account to make.

If the letter is a document on your phone

Most things people need to mail already exist as a file: a signed form, a scan, an email attachment, a Word document. Save it or export it as a PDF, then attach it on the order form. Most phones can scan a paper document to a PDF with the built-in camera or notes app, which is the quickest way to turn a paper form into a file. The order page shows the exact printed page before you pay, so you can check that nothing is cut off.

Who uses it

  • People outside Canada who need a letter posted from a Canadian address. See how to send a letter to Canada from abroad.
  • Students and renters who do not own a printer and need to mail a form or a notice.
  • Older relatives and anyone who cannot get to a post office and would rather send a letter from home. See send a letter from home.
  • Anyone with a deadline and a document that only exists on a phone or in an email.

What to check before you pay

  • The recipient address is complete, with the postal code. The address formatter shows the correct form.
  • The preview matches what you want printed, including page breaks.
  • You have chosen colour only if you need it. Black and white is the default.
  • If a deadline depends on the letter arriving, allow for delivery time. Standard mail usually takes 2 to 9 business days after we mail it, and Canada Post does not guarantee a date.

What it costs and what is included

$6 to any address in Canada or the United States. That covers two pages printed in black and white, the envelope and the postage. Colour is $1 more per order. Each page after the second is $0.55, or $0.75 in colour. A letter can run to five pages. Sending to the UK or Australia is $9, and $15 to other countries.

When mailing it yourself is cheaper. If you can print at home and have a stamp, mailing the letter yourself costs less. The online route is for the cases where there is no printer, no envelope, no time for the trip, or you are not in Canada.
