How to mail CRA tax forms from outside Canada

To mail CRA tax forms from outside Canada: upload your completed forms to Letterhome, enter the correct CRA address for your province, and we print and mail them domestically within Canada.

Updated May 2026

Short answer: if you're outside Canada and need to mail tax forms or correspondence to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), you can do it entirely online — no trip home and no hunt for international postage. Upload your completed forms, enter the correct CRA tax centre address, and Letterhome prints them in full colour and mails them from inside Canada via Canada Post, usually within one business day.

The cost is $10 CAD to any Canadian address, including CRA tax centres. That includes up to 5 documents, full-colour printing, the envelope, and Canadian postage.

Important: the correct CRA mailing address depends on your form and your province of residence. Always confirm your tax centre's current address on canada.ca before ordering. Letterhome mails to the address you provide and does not give tax advice.
