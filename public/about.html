About Letterhome

A small mail service, run from Canada.

Letterhome prints and posts letters and documents from inside Canada on your behalf. Each order is handled by a person — checked for obvious problems, printed in full colour, packed into a stamped envelope, and dropped at the post office.

Why we built this

The gap we're filling.

If you live outside Canada, mailing something back home is harder than it should be. The CRA wants signed paper. Family expect a letter on the right day. Banks and accountants keep sending mail to addresses you no longer use. From Berlin, Bangkok or Auckland, none of that is easy to deal with.

Letterhome is the workaround. You write the letter, attach any documents, pay, and we mail it from Canada — just like dropping it off at a Canada Post box yourself. We also mail outwards from Canada to 160+ countries, so it works the other way around too.

Orders are checked before printing for things that would stop delivery — bad addresses, unreadable PDFs, obvious abuse or illegal content. Not for what you wrote. Your letter and attachments are deleted within 7 days of mailing.

A small operation, run from Canada. Every order is reviewed and prepared by hand before it goes in the post.

How we operate.

01

Hand-checked before printing

Every order is reviewed by a person before it's printed — for addressing errors, unreadable attachments, or anything else that would stop the mail from getting through. If there's a problem, we email you before charging or printing.

02

We don't keep your letters

Your letter and any attachments are deleted from our systems within 7 days of mailing. We keep a basic order record — date, amount, your email — for Canadian tax purposes. Not what you wrote.

03

Flat pricing

$10 to a Canadian address, $20 to anywhere else. The price includes up to 5 documents of full-colour printing, the envelope, postage, and the trip to the post office. No per-page charges or surprise add-ons.

04

Payments through Stripe

Payments are processed by Stripe. We don't see or store your card details, and your financial information doesn't touch our systems.

05

Canada Post for delivery

Letters go through Canada Post's network with the same postage and service level you'd get if you'd mailed it yourself from a Canadian address.

06

Direct replies

No ticket queues. If something goes wrong with your order, the person who replies is the person who handled it. We aim to respond within one business day.
