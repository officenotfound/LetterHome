About Letterhome

You write a letter online. We print it, put it in an envelope and mail it from inside Canada. It's for people who live abroad and still need something on paper to reach Canada.

I'm Jeffrey. In 2026 I was in South America and needed to get one form to the CRA. Getting a letter into the Canadian mail from abroad was far harder than it should have been, so I built the service I wanted.

Jeffrey George, founder

Checked before printing
We check the address and your files first. If something is wrong, we email you before we charge or print.
One price
$6 to Canada or the US, $9 to the UK or Australia, $15 anywhere else. Printing, the envelope and postage are included.
Private
We don't read or share your letter unless our automatic check flags illegal content. We keep an order record (date, amount, email) for tax, never what you wrote. Ask us to delete it any time.
Safe payment
Stripe handles your card. We never see the number.
Questions
Email support@letterhome.ca. Whoever answers handled your order, usually within one business day.
