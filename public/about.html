About Letterhome

One person. One printer. Every letter.

The origin

Built for a gap nobody warned you about.

Living outside Canada comes with a specific paperwork problem. The CRA still wants signed documents. Banks still send statements by post. Your mum still appreciates a real card. And you're in Berlin, or Bangkok, or Auckland — with no practical way to put something in the Canadian mail.

Letterhome started as a solution to that problem. The premise is simple: you write the letter, we take care of everything between your screen and the recipient's letterbox.

There's no algorithm deciding what gets mailed. No automated quality check. Every order goes through me before it leaves. That's by design.

I started this because I needed it myself. The service I wanted didn't exist — so I built it, and I still run every order personally. That's not a limitation. That's the point.

How we operate.

01

Human review, every time

Every order is read and checked by a real person before printing. If something looks off — wrong address, garbled content, anything — you get a message before anything is printed or charged.

02

No unnecessary data storage

Your letter content is used to print your letter and then deleted. We don't keep it, analyse it, or use it for anything else. The order record we retain is the minimum required by Canadian tax law.

03

Flat, honest pricing

$10 domestic. $20 international. That's it. No per-page charges, no platform fees, no surprise add-ons at checkout. The price you see is the price you pay.

04

Stripe for payments

We don't see or store your card details. Payment goes through Stripe — the same processor used by millions of businesses worldwide. Your financial information never touches our systems.

05

Canada Post for delivery

Your letter enters Canada Post's network — the same postal system your letter would have used if you'd mailed it yourself from a Canadian address. Same postmark, same service, same reliability.

06

Direct accountability

There's no support ticket queue. If something goes wrong with your order, you're talking to the same person who handled it. Problems get fixed the same day, not routed through a helpdesk.

Ready to send something home?

It takes about three minutes. Write your letter, pay, and we'll handle the rest.
