Where to mail BC vital statistics documents

Mail your completed application to the Vital Statistics Agency, PO Box 9657 Stn Prov Govt, Victoria, BC, V8W 9P3. This single P.O. Box, published on the official gov.bc.ca page, handles mailed birth, marriage, death, adoption, name-change, genealogy, and statutory declaration requests.

Updated October 2026

Newcomers and Canadians abroad who need a BC-issued certificate — for a passport renewal, a credential assessment, or a sponsorship application — can order by mail using the form that matches their request. All of them go to the same Victoria P.O. Box, which makes it one of the more straightforward vital-records addresses to get right.

Source: gov.bc.ca — Order certificates and copies

Match the form to the request: BC Vital Statistics uses a different numbered form for each certificate or service — birth, marriage, death, adoption, name change, genealogy, and statutory declarations each have their own. The P.O. Box is the same for all of them; the form is not.

Compare your options: the official fee schedule lists $27 per mailed certificate, with online ordering by credit card and in-person Service BC counters also available. Mail is typically the route for applicants who can't use the online service or need to attach supporting documents.

Why this matters for applicants abroad

BC's Vital Statistics Agency doesn't take these requests by email, so the completed form, payment, and any supporting documents have to arrive as physical mail at this Victoria P.O. Box. For someone living outside Canada, Letterhome prints the signed application you upload and mails it from inside Canada by Canada Post, so it reaches the Agency the same way a form mailed by a BC resident would.
