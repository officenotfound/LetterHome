Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

Can I use Letterhome if I'm not in Canada?

Yes — that's exactly who we built it for. The whole process happens online, so as long as you have an internet connection and a way to pay, you can send mail from anywhere in the world. You don't need a Canadian address, a Canadian credit card, or a VPN. We accept international cards through Stripe.

Where does the letter actually get mailed from?

Letters are printed and mailed from within Canada. That means a Canadian postmark, Canadian postage, and full access to Canada Post's domestic and international delivery network. For the recipient, it looks identical to a letter mailed from down the street.

What's the difference between domestic and international pricing?

Domestic is $10 CAD per letter — anywhere within Canada. International is $20 CAD per letter — anywhere outside Canada, to over 160 countries. Both prices include everything: printing, paper, envelope, postage, and delivery. No per-page charges up to 5 pages.

How long does delivery take?

Once we receive your order, it's printed and mailed within one business day. After that, mail within Canada typically arrives within 2 weeks. International mail typically takes up to 4 weeks, depending on the destination country. Over 99% of our letters arrive within these windows.

Can I attach documents like PDFs or Word files?

Yes. You can attach PDF or Word documents to your letter. These are printed alongside your message and included in the same envelope. This is what makes Letterhome useful for tax forms, signed contracts, immigration paperwork, and other documents that need to physically arrive somewhere.

Is the content of my letter kept private?

Yes. Letters are reviewed only briefly to screen for prohibited content (threats, scams, illegal material) before being printed. We don't store, read, or share the contents of your letters for any other purpose. Once your letter has been mailed, the digital file and attachments are deleted from our system within 7 days. We retain a basic order record (date, amount, your email) as required by Canadian tax law, but not the letter content itself. Payments are processed securely through Stripe — we never see your card details.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can send a one-off letter without signing up — just write, pay, and you're done. If you send mail regularly (especially for business or tax purposes), creating an account lets you save addresses, track past orders, and reuse templates.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe, including international cards. Prices are charged in Canadian dollars; your bank will convert at the prevailing exchange rate. More payment options (PayPal, Square, and others) are coming soon.

What happens if my letter doesn't arrive?

Letter mail is highly reliable — over 99% of our letters arrive within the expected window. Because standard letter mail doesn't have tracking, we can't intercept or trace individual pieces in transit. All sales are final. If your letter hasn't arrived within 2 weeks (domestic) or 4 weeks (international), contact us — we'll verify the details and do our best to make it right. For time-sensitive legal or government documents, ask us about registered mail options.

Can I send marketing or bulk mail?

Yes, in small batches. If you need to send the same letter or postcard to multiple Canadian addresses, get in touch and we can arrange bulk pricing. For larger volume direct-mail campaigns (500+ pieces), we'll point you to a partner better suited to that scale.
