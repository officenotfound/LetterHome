How it works

From your screen to the mailbox.

Everything happens online. The letter gets mailed from Canada.

i Step One

Write your letter

Type your message, enter the recipient's address, and attach any documents (PDF or Word). A return address is optional, anonymous sends are supported.

ii Step Two

We print and prepare it

Your letter and any attachments are printed in black & white by default (colour available) on letter-size paper, folded, sealed, and stamped with Canadian postage.

iii Step Three

It goes in the mail

We drop your letter at Canada Post within one business day. Delivery typically takes up to two weeks within Canada and up to four weeks internationally.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I use Letterhome if I'm not in Canada?

Yes. The whole process is online, so you can submit an order from anywhere with an internet connection. You don't need a Canadian address or a Canadian credit card. International cards are accepted through Stripe; you're charged in Canadian dollars and your bank converts at its exchange rate.

Where does the letter actually get mailed from?

Inside Canada. Letters carry Canadian postage and go out through Canada Post. To the recipient, the envelope is indistinguishable from one you would have mailed yourself at a Canada Post box.

What's the difference between domestic and international pricing?

Two prices: $10 CAD for letters to a Canadian address, $20 CAD for letters to any of 160+ international destinations. Either price covers up to 5 documents of black & white printing by default (colour available) on letter-size paper, the envelope, postage, and the trip to the post office.

How long does delivery take?

We print and mail within one business day of payment. After that, Canada Post's standard lettermail typically takes up to 2 weeks within Canada and up to 4 weeks internationally, depending on the destination. Tracked or registered options are available for an additional fee, email sales@letterhome.ca before placing your order for a quote.

Can I attach documents like PDFs or Word files?

Yes. Attach up to 5 PDF or Word files (10 MB each). They're printed in black & white by default (colour available) and included in the same envelope as your letter. This is how most people use the service for tax forms, contracts, and immigration paperwork.

Is the content of my letter kept private?

Yes. Orders are checked for things that would stop the mail from going through: bad addresses, unreadable files, obvious abuse or illegal content. We don't check for what you wrote. Your letter and any attachments are deleted from our systems within 7 days of mailing. We keep a basic order record (date, amount, your email) for Canadian tax purposes, but not the content of what you sent. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card details.
