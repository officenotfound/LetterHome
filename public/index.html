Common questions.

Can I use Letterhome if I'm not in Canada?

Yes. Everything happens online, and you don't need a Canadian address or credit card. International cards are accepted through Stripe. You pay in your own currency at checkout.

Where does the letter actually get mailed from?

Letters to Canada go out through Canada Post as regular Lettermail. Letters to the US, UK and Australia are printed and mailed in those countries. Everywhere else is mailed from Canada.

What does it cost to mail to Canada, the United States, or elsewhere?

$6 to Canada or the United States, $9 to the UK or Australia, and $15 to 160+ other countries. Each price covers 2 black & white pages, the envelope and postage. Colour is +$1, and extra pages are $0.55 each ($0.75 in colour).

How long does delivery take?

We print and mail your letter within two business days. Delivery usually takes 1 to 2 weeks in Canada and up to 4 weeks abroad, depending on the destination. Standard letters do not include tracking or proof of delivery.

Can I attach documents like PDFs or Word files?

Yes. Attach up to 5 PDF or Word files (10 MB each), up to 5 printed pages in total (4 if you only upload a PDF). They go in the same envelope as your letter.

Is the content of my letter kept private?

Yes. An automatic check on our own server looks for illegal content, and we do not read or share what you wrote unless it flags something. You can ask us to delete your letter and personal information at any time. We keep a basic order record (date, amount, your email) for tax purposes.
