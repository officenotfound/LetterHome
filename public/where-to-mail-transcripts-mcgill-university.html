Where to mail transcripts to McGill University

Most undergraduate official transcripts and admission documents go to McGill's Enrolment Services at 3415 McTavish Street, Room MS 13, Montreal, QC, H3A 0C8. This is the address McGill's own undergraduate admissions site publishes, and it covers Arts, Science, Engineering, Management, Nursing, and most other undergraduate faculties.

Updated October 2026

If you're applying to McGill from outside Canada and your school issues official transcripts only on paper, you'll need to mail that sealed document into Canada once you have your McGill application ID.

Faculty-specific exceptions: Medicine and Law run their own separate admissions offices with different mailing addresses. Always confirm the address on your specific faculty's admissions page, since this general address is for most undergraduate programs, not every one.

McGill asks applicants to wait until they have their 9-digit McGill ID before mailing anything, and not to send paper copies of documents already submitted electronically.
