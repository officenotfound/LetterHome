    Send a fax to the US or Canada

    A fax to any US or Canadian fax number costs $2.50 for up to 10 pages, plus $0.25 for each extra page. We email you when it has been delivered.

    How it works

    1. Upload and pay

      Add your document and the fax number, see the exact price, and pay by card.

    2. We send it

      If the line is busy or does not answer, we try twice more. If it still does not go through, we email you about what happens next.

    3. You get a confirmation

      We email you when the fax machine has received every page, with the time it was delivered.

    What we can send

    • Faxes to US and Canadian fax numbers. Toll-free numbers are not accepted.
    • PDF, JPG and PNG files. A password-protected PDF has to be saved without its password first.

    Your document

    Your file is stored privately, is never shown to anyone outside Letterhome except the fax service that sends it, and is deleted 24 hours after the fax is finished.

    Common questions

    How do I know it arrived?

    We email you once the receiving fax machine has accepted every page, with the date and time. Keep that email as your record.

    What if the fax does not go through?

    If the line is busy or does not answer, we try twice more. If it still fails, we email you about what happens next.

    Can I fax a government office?

    Yes, to any Canadian or US fax number. Check the office's current fax number on its own website before you send.
