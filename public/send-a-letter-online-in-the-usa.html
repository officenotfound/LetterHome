You don't need a printer, a book of stamps or a trip to the post office to mail a letter. Here's how it works when you write it online, and what to know before you do.

The short version

  1. Type your letter

    Write it right on the page, or attach a PDF or Word file. Up to 5 files, and photos print in color if you pick color.

  2. Enter the address

    Name, street, city, state and ZIP code. For a Canadian address, add the province and postal code.

  3. Pay and relax

    Every address is verified, then it is printed, put in an envelope and mailed. You get an email when it goes out.

What it costs

Letters start at $3.00. The USA page shows every price. That price covers printing, the envelope and postage for up to 2 black-and-white pages. Color is $0.50 extra, and each extra page is $0.50 ($1 in color).

Who it's for

  • Anyone who'd rather send a real letter than another text.
  • People without a printer at home, like most of us these days.
  • Students, travelers and anyone far from a post office.
  • Anyone mailing a form that has to be on paper.

Things to know first

  • Standard mail has no tracking and may not carry a dated postmark, so it isn't proof of mailing. See our proof of mailing guide.
  • Use the full ZIP code. If you only know the five digits, that's fine.
  • Want it to arrive on a specific day, like a birthday? Schedule it for later.
  • We don't check what's in your letter or your forms, so look them over before you send.

Common questions

Can I really mail a letter without stamps?

Yes. Postage is included in the price, and we handle the envelope and the mailing.

Do I need a printer?

No. We print it for you.

Can I mail to Canada from the U.S.?

Yes. Add the Canadian address with the province and postal code. See mailing to Canada from the U.S.

How long does it take?

It depends on where it goes. The order page shows the delivery time for your address before you pay.
