Where to mail an Ontario name change application

Mail your completed name change application to ServiceOntario, Office of the Registrar General, P.O. Box 3000, 189 Red River Road, Thunder Bay, Ontario, P7B 0B3. This is the exact address published on the official ontario.ca change-of-name page.

Updated October 2026

A legal name change in Ontario — common for newcomers aligning documents with a new legal identity, or for a marriage or divorce — can't be submitted entirely online. The form, supporting documents, and any payment have to go by mail to the Office of the Registrar General in Thunder Bay. If you're outside Canada without a return address, Letterhome can print and mail the completed package for you.

Use the right P.O. Box: the Office of the Registrar General processes different services at different P.O. Boxes on the same Thunder Bay street address. Birth certificate requests go to P.O. Box 4600, not P.O. Box 3000 — always follow the box number printed on your specific form.

Fees may be waived: ontario.ca states that if you're eligible, name change fees are waived. Check the current eligibility criteria on the official page before you mail your payment.
