Where to mail documents to Dalhousie University

Mailed transcripts and records documents for Dalhousie go to the Registrar's Office, Room 130, Henry Hicks Administration Building, PO Box 15000, Halifax, NS, B3H 4R2. This is the mailing address Dalhousie's own Registrar's Office publishes for records correspondence.

Updated October 2026

If you're applying to or studying at Dalhousie from outside Canada and your previous school can only issue a paper transcript, or you need to mail a signed records form, it needs to reach this Halifax address through Canadian mail.

Admissions correspondence is usually by email, not mail: Dalhousie says official acceptance letters and most application communication come by email from admissions@dal.ca. Mail is mainly for transcript requests, records forms, and documents your previous school can only issue on paper.

Check your faculty first: some faculties, including Medicine and the School of Health Administration, publish their own separate admissions mailing or courier address. The Registrar's Office address above is the general one for records and transcripts.
