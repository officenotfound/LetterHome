Pick your envelope, enter the weight of the sealed letter, and the calculator gives the Canada Post domestic letter rate. Don't know the weight? Enter the number of sheets below and it estimates it.

Estimate the weight from the number of sheets

A sheet counts once whether it is printed on one side or both. These weights are typical, so weigh the real letter on a kitchen scale if it is close to 30 g or 50 g.
