How many pages can you mail with one stamp in Canada?

Rates checked November 2026

One $1.44 stamp covers about five sheets of 20 lb paper in a plain #10 envelope, and up to nine sheets with the $1.75 rate. The chart below shows the weight and postage from one to twelve sheets. Use the postage calculator if your paper or envelope is different.

Heavier paper

With 24 lb (90 gsm) paper at about 5.4 g a sheet, four sheets plus the envelope come to 27.6 g, and five sheets come to 33 g, which moves the letter into the $1.75 rate. So the heavier the paper, the sooner you cross the 30 g line.

Why the weight matters

Canada Post prices the sealed envelope and everything in it. A letter under 30 g is $1.44, up to 50 g is $1.75, and over 50 g it moves to the large letter rates. A letter that is too thick (over 5 mm) or too large (over 245 by 156 mm) is a large letter even if it is light. The details are in postage for a large letter in Canada.
