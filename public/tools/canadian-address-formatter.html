How a Canadian address is built

The recipient goes on the first line. The second line is the delivery address: the unit number, a hyphen, the street number and the street name with its type and direction abbreviated, as in 4-123 MAIN ST NW. The third line holds the municipality, the two-letter province or territory code and the postal code, separated by a space, with no comma. A letter from another country adds CANADA on a fourth line.
