Where to mail Quebec vital statistics documents

Mail your completed application to Directeur de l'état civil, Demande de documents (DCCA), 2535, boulevard Laurier, Québec (Québec)  G1V 5C6. This address, printed on every current Application for a Certificate or Copy of an Act form, handles mailed birth, marriage/civil union, and death certificate requests.

Updated October 2026

Newcomers and Canadians abroad who need a Quebec-issued certificate — for a passport renewal, a credential assessment, or a sponsorship application — can order one by mail using the form for their event type. All three forms are mailed to the same Québec City address.

Only for existing records: this address and form order a certificate or copy of an act that's already entered in the Quebec register of civil status. A Quebec legal name change is a separate application process, not a mail-in certificate request — this page does not cover it.

Compare your options: a mailed application costs $55.00 CAD for a certificate or $64.25 CAD for a copy of an act at normal processing (about 10 business days, excluding event-registration and delivery time). Accelerated mail processing costs $82.25 CAD and runs about 3 business days. Filing online through DEClic! is cheaper — mail is the option when you don't have internet access or a Government Authentication Service identifier.

What to send with your application

By mail, the Directeur de l'état civil asks for photocopies — not originals — of two identity documents issued by two separate organizations: a valid photo ID with signature, and a valid proof of home address. The form must be signed and dated, and payment (credit card authorization, cheque, or money order payable to "Directeur de l'état civil") included. Applications sent from outside Canada by private courier must declare a shipment value of $0, or delivery will be refused.

Why this matters for applicants abroad

The Directeur de l'état civil doesn't take mailed certificate requests by email, so the signed form, ID photocopies, and payment have to arrive as physical mail at this Québec City address. For someone living outside Canada, Letterhome prints the application you upload and mails it from inside Canada by Canada Post, so it reaches the Directeur the same way a form mailed by a Quebec resident would.
