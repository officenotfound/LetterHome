Cheapest way to send a letter to Canada

The cheapest way to send a letter to Canada from abroad depends on your country — but Letterhome at $10 CAD is often cheaper than courier and comparable to standard international post, with the benefit of domestic Canadian delivery speed.

Updated June 2026

The cheapest option depends entirely on where you are and what you already own. If you're sitting in Canada right now with a working printer, a box of envelopes, and a book of stamps — you're already set. The math is simple: $1.24 per letter (booklet rate). But if you're outside Canada, or you don't have a printer, or you need to send multiple documents in colour, the calculation changes quickly.

Here's an honest look at the numbers, with no upselling.

Option 2: Mail from outside Canada (international postage)

If you're abroad, you can send a letter to Canada using your local postal service — but international postage is more expensive and slower than domestic.

International mail to Canada also passes through customs handling and travels on international routes, which typically means slower delivery times than a letter mailed from within Canada.

Option 3: Print-and-mail service (from anywhere)

A print-and-mail service prints your letter or documents inside Canada and posts them with Canadian postage. The total cost is $10 CAD to any Canadian address — that's printing, envelope, postage, and delivery, all in.

This option makes sense when one or more of these applies:

You're outside Canada and don't own a printer. You need to send multiple documents in full colour. You don't have access to a local post office, or the international postage rates from your country are high. You'd otherwise need to ask someone in Canada to mail it for you. Or you just want it handled without making a trip.
