Send a letter from home

To send a letter from home without going to the post office: use Letterhome — upload your document online, enter the address, and we handle the printing, stamping, and mailing entirely on your behalf.

Updated June 2026

You don't need a printer, stamps, or an envelope to send a real physical letter. Most people assume mailing a letter means owning all the right supplies and making a trip to a mailbox or post office. It doesn't. If you have an internet connection and a way to pay online, you can send a letter from your couch.

With Letterhome, you write your letter or upload a document online. We print it in full colour, seal it in an envelope, apply postage, and deposit it with Canada Post — usually within one business day. You stay home.

Who sends letters this way

People who never got around to buying stamps. Stamps are one of those things you only need occasionally and never have when you need them. This skips that entirely.

People between printers. Printer broke, ran out of ink, or never bought one — none of that matters when the printing is handled for you.

People outside Canada. If you're abroad and need to mail something to a Canadian address, going to your local post office means international postage, customs forms, and longer delivery. Ordering through Letterhome means the letter goes out from inside Canada as domestic mail.

People who are ill, elderly, or have mobility constraints. A letter that used to require a trip out of the house can now be sent entirely from bed or a desk chair.

People who just want it handled. Some things are worth paying a few dollars to not think about.
