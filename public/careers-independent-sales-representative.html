About Letterhome

Letterhome is a Canadian online mail service. Businesses and individuals upload a letter or type it online, and we print it, put it in an envelope and mail it. Customers include landlords and property managers sending notices, law and immigration offices mailing client documents, small businesses sending invoices and statements, and real estate agents sending postcards. We serve Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom.

About the role

We are looking for independent sales representatives to find and sign business clients who send mail regularly. You set your own hours and work from wherever you like, anywhere in the world. This is a commission-only contract role: you are paid only when a client you referred pays us.

What you will do

  • Find businesses that mail regularly, such as property managers, law offices, immigration consultants, accountants, clinics and real estate agents.
  • Explain how Letterhome works and send interested clients to our business quote form, or introduce them to us directly.
  • Follow up with leads and keep a simple record of who you have contacted.
  • Follow our outreach guidelines: no spam, no bulk unsolicited email, and no claims we have not approved.

How you are paid

  • 15% commission on everything a client you referred pays us in their first 12 months.
  • Paid monthly, by e-transfer or PayPal, for client payments received in the previous month.
  • Commission is tracked with a personal referral link or code and confirmed with you each month.
  • No fees, no starter kit and nothing to buy. You never pay us anything.

What we are looking for

  • Experience in B2B sales, lead generation or account management.
  • Clear written and spoken English. Spanish or French is a plus.
  • Comfortable working on your own and setting your own schedule.
  • A professional manner on email, phone and LinkedIn.
  • Your own computer and phone, with a reliable internet connection.

What we provide

  • Product training, pricing sheets and a sales one-pager.
  • A personal referral link and code.
  • Direct contact with the founder for quotes and questions.

Contract details

  • You will be an independent contractor, not an employee, and responsible for your own taxes in your country.
  • Fully remote. Work from anywhere in the world, on your own equipment.
  • No minimum hours or quotas.
  • Either side can end the agreement with 14 days’ notice. Commission already earned is still paid.

Apply

Send a short note about your sales experience and the types of businesses you would approach. We reply to every application by email.

We only use your resume to review your application.

We never charge applicants for anything. If anyone asks you to pay to work with Letterhome, it is not us.
