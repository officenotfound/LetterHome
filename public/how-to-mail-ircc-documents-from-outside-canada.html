How to mail IRCC immigration documents from outside Canada

To mail IRCC documents from outside Canada: upload your PDFs to Letterhome, enter the correct IRCC mailing address, and we print and post them from inside Canada — no international mail required.

Updated June 2026

IRCC still requires physical mail for many immigration applications — and being outside Canada doesn't get you out of that. Citizenship applications, certain permanent residence streams, and supporting document requests all have specific mailing addresses. If you're living abroad and need to send physical paperwork to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, here's how to do it without getting on a plane.

Letterhome prints your documents at a Canadian facility, seals them in an envelope, and mails them from inside Canada using Canada Post. The envelope carries a Canadian postmark and reaches IRCC's processing centre as domestic mail.

Check originals requirements first. For most supporting documents, IRCC accepts colour printed copies. But some items — original police certificates, original identity documents, notarized copies with a physical seal — must be genuine originals and cannot be reprinted. Always read the specific instructions for your application type at canada.ca/ircc before submitting.

How to send it

  • Prepare your documentsSign your forms if required. Save everything as PDF files — one file per document type, or combine them in order. Check that your documents are complete and match the checklist for your application.
  • Find the correct IRCC mailing addressIRCC routes mail to different processing centres depending on your application stream. Look up the address in the application guide on the IRCC website. Using the wrong address can delay processing.
  • Upload to LetterhomeAttach up to 5 PDF or Word files to your order (10 MB each). Add a cover letter as its own file if your application requires one. Everything prints in full colour.
  • Enter the IRCC addressPaste in the full IRCC processing centre address as the recipient. Leave your return address blank or enter a valid address if you want undeliverable mail returned to you.
  • Pay and we handle the restAny major card from any country. Your documents are printed, sealed, and mailed from Canada — usually within one business day of your order.

What documents people send

Common submissions that customers mail through Letterhome to Canadian immigration and government addresses:

Citizenship applications. The citizenship application process still involves physical submissions in many cases, including supporting documents and the application form itself.

Permanent residence supporting documents. Some PR streams request original or certified supporting documents by mail as part of the application process.

Sponsorship applications. Family sponsorship paperwork often requires physical submission with signed forms.

Document requests and correspondence. Responding to IRCC requests for additional documentation, or sending a cover letter to accompany something sent electronically.

Provincial nominee supporting documents. Some PNP streams require mailed documentation to provincial offices at Canadian addresses.
