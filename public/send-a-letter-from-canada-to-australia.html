Dates that matter in Australia

A letter timed for one of these days is the one that gets read twice. The order page shows the delivery time for their address before you pay, so you can plan the date.

  • Australia Day, 26 January.
  • Anzac Day, 25 April.
  • Mother's Day, the second Sunday of May.
  • Father's Day, the first Sunday of September.
  • Boxing Day, 26 December.

You can write a birthday or holiday letter ahead of time and use schedule for later so it lands on the day.

Words that change between Canada and Australia

Your relatives will read it in their own English, so a few words land differently.

  • Mother: Mom in Canada, Mum in Australia.
  • Postal code: postal code in Canada, postcode in Australia.
  • Apartment: apartment in Canada, unit or flat in Australia.
  • Where the letter lands: mailbox in Canada, letterbox in Australia.
  • Time off: vacation in Canada, holiday in Australia.
  • Biscuit: cookie in Canada, biscuit in Australia.
  • Pavement: sidewalk in Canada, footpath in Australia.

Dates trip people up too. In Canada they are usually written in either order, so you will see 12/25/2026, 25/12/2026 and 2026-12-25, while in Australia they are written day first, like 25/12/2026. Spell the month out, like 25 December 2026, and nobody has to guess.

The seasons are reversed. When it is summer in Canada, it is winter in Australia, so a letter about the weather lands as a little window into another world.
