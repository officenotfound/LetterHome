How to send a letter to Canada from abroad

To send a letter to Canada from abroad: upload your document to Letterhome, enter the Canadian recipient address, and pay, we print and mail it from inside Canada via Canada Post within one business day.

Updated August 2026

Short answer: you don't need to be in Canada, or have a Canadian address or a Canadian credit card, to mail a letter to a Canadian address. Use an online service that prints and posts from within Canada. With Letterhome you write or upload your letter, enter the recipient's Canadian address, and pay by card from any country. We print it in black & white by default (colour available) in Canada, seal it in an envelope, apply Canadian postage, and drop it with Canada Post, usually within one business day.
