Common questions

How long does a credit bureau have to investigate?

Both bureaus are generally required to investigate within a set number of days of receiving your written dispute. Check the current timeline on the bureau's own site.

Do I need to dispute with both Equifax and TransUnion?

If the error appears on both reports, yes, they operate independently and don't automatically share dispute outcomes.

What if the dispute is denied?

You can request the bureau add a statement of dispute to your file, and in some cases escalate to a provincial or federal regulator, depending on the nature of the error.

How much does it cost to mail?

$10 CAD for a domestic Canadian address, covering printing, postage, and mailing.
