Common questions

Is there a minimum order?

No. A single letter works from the send page. Batches of ten or more pieces are quoted individually so you get the best total.

How long does it take?

Pieces go to print within two business days of your approval. Delivery is typically up to two weeks within the destination country and up to four weeks internationally.

Can I use my own files?

Yes. Send PDF or Word files, or plain text, and we lay out and print them. Colour printing is available.

Do I need an account?

No. We can also set up a business account for regular senders so you can upload batches yourself. Ask when you request your quote.

What do you check before printing?

Every recipient address is checked for delivery problems, and every batch is reviewed before it is printed. We do not mail threats, scams or illegal content.
