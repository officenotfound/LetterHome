USPS postage rates
in 2026

A stamped 1 ounce letter costs 82 cents and a metered letter 78 cents. A postcard costs 65 cents and a large envelope $1.69. Each extra ounce on a letter adds 29 cents, and Certified Mail adds $5.55.

Updated November 2026

First-Class letters

A stamped letter weighing up to 1 ounce costs 82 cents. Each extra ounce adds 29 cents. If the postage is printed by a meter or bought online, the price is 4 cents lower. A letter can weigh up to 3.5 ounces. Anything heavier is priced as a large envelope.

To qualify as a letter, the envelope must be rectangular, between 3.5 by 5 inches and 6.125 by 11.5 inches, and no more than a quarter inch thick. Square, rigid or lumpy envelopes cost more because machines handle them poorly, so ask at the counter if yours is unusual.

Postcards

A postcard costs 65 cents. A Forever stamp also works, but it pays more than a postcard needs. A card that is larger than the postcard size limit is priced as a letter.

Large envelopes (flats)

A large envelope costs $1.69 for the first ounce and $1.98 for two ounces. The price keeps rising by about 30 cents for each extra ounce, up to 13 ounces. The full step-by-step table is in the USPS price list linked below. Letters over 3.5 ounces and envelopes larger than the letter size are priced as flats.

As an example, a one-ounce letter sent with Certified Mail costs $6.37 ($0.82 plus $5.55). With an electronic return receipt it is $9.28, and with a hard copy receipt it is $11.02. What Certified Mail does and does not prove is covered in its own guide.

Does USPS deliver on Sunday?

Not for regular mail. Letters sent as First-Class Mail are not delivered on Sundays. Priority Mail Express is the exception: USPS offers it seven days a week, with a Sunday and holiday fee on top of the normal price. There is also no regular delivery on federal holidays. The next ones are Thanksgiving on November 26, Christmas on December 25 and New Year's Day on January 1, 2027.

How often prices change

USPS changed its prices on July 12, 2026, and a Forever stamp went from 78 to 82 cents. Our stamp price guide covers the history and why old Forever stamps still work. Prices are published in Notice 123, so check it before a large mailing.

Common questions

How much is a stamp to mail a letter?

A stamped one-ounce First-Class letter costs 82 cents. A metered or online letter costs 78 cents.

How much is a postcard stamp in 2026?

A postcard costs 65 cents to mail in the United States.

How much does it cost to mail a letter over one ounce?

Each additional ounce adds 29 cents. A stamped two-ounce letter costs $1.11 and a three-ounce letter costs $1.40, up to 3.5 ounces.

How much is a large envelope to mail?

A large envelope (flat) costs $1.69 for the first ounce and $1.98 for two ounces, with a further increase of about 30 cents per ounce.

Does USPS deliver on Sunday?

Not regular mail. First-Class letters are not delivered on Sundays. Priority Mail Express is delivered seven days a week for an extra fee.
