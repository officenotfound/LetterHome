Write your letter now, check the schedule option, and pick the date you want it mailed. We factor in typical Canada Post delivery time so it lands close to the date you actually care about, not the date you happened to sit down and write it.

How it works

  1. Write your letter as usual

    Type it or attach a PDF, add the recipient's address, same as any other order.

  2. Check "Schedule for later"

    This adds $0.63 to your order. A date picker appears for you to choose your mailing date.

  3. Pick your date

    Choose the date you want it mailed. For a specific arrival date like a birthday, mail a few days ahead of it to account for delivery time.

  4. We hold it and mail it on schedule

    Your letter is printed and mailed on the date you chose, going out via Canada Post from inside Canada.

Good uses for scheduling

Birthdays and anniversaries where you want the letter to actually land on or near the day, not whenever you happened to write it. Also useful if you're writing several letters at once and want them staggered rather than all arriving the same week.

Common questions

How far in advance can I write it?

Write and submit it anytime, then choose your future mailing date at checkout.

Can I cancel or change the date after ordering?

Email support@letterhome.ca with your order number as soon as possible, changes are only possible before the letter has been printed.

Will it arrive exactly on the date I choose?

We mail on your chosen date, but Canada Post delivery time after that is standard lettermail speed, mail a few days ahead of your target date to allow for it.

How much does scheduling cost?

An extra $0.63 on top of your base letter price.
