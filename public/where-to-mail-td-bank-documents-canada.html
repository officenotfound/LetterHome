Where to mail signed TD documents from outside Canada

TD publishes a Customer Care mailing address at its Toronto-Dominion Centre head office for general correspondence and complaint escalations. If a specific branch or newcomer banking advisor hasn't given you a mailing address for your signed paperwork, this is the general address TD itself lists on its official contact page.

Updated October 2026

TD, like the other major Canadian banks, runs programs to help newcomers to Canada start a banking relationship before they land, but the account itself is finalized through a specific branch, not by mailing documents to head office cold. If your advisor or branch has given you an address, use it. Otherwise, TD's published Customer Care address is the verified general fallback.
