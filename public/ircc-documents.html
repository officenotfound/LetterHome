Common IRCC mailing addresses

IRCC uses different addresses depending on application type. Always verify the current address on the IRCC website before submitting — addresses change and using the wrong one will delay your application.

Key processing centres include locations in Sydney NS, Mississauga ON, Ottawa ON, and Vegreville AB. The official address directory is at canada.ca — find an IRCC office.

Common questions

Can I mail IRCC documents from outside Canada?

Yes. IRCC accepts mail from international addresses, but the mailing date and mailing origin depend on the postal service you use. Using Letterhome, your documents are mailed from inside Canada via Canada Post, so they arrive with a Canadian mailing date regardless of where you are in the world.

Will IRCC accept documents printed from a PDF?

For many document types, yes — sponsorship letters, cover letters, forms, and financial documents are routinely accepted as printed copies. Whether originals are required depends entirely on the specific document and application type. Always check the document checklist for your application on the IRCC website.

How many pages can I include?

You can attach up to 5 files per order. If your submission has more documents, place a second order and note in the letter body that it is a continuation. Each order covers standard lettermail postage, which typically handles up to 30 grams — roughly 6 to 8 sheets of paper.

How much does it cost?

$20 CAD for mail to a Canadian address (IRCC offices are all in Canada). That covers printing, postage, the envelope, and mailing via Canada Post. No subscription or account required.

Do you offer certified mail or registered mail?

Currently we mail via standard Canada Post lettermail, which does not include tracking or delivery confirmation. If your application requires proof of delivery, we recommend adding a cover letter noting your order confirmation number and mailing date, and following up with IRCC directly after allowing adequate delivery time.

I work with immigration clients — can I use this for them?

Yes. Immigration consultants (RCICs), lawyers, and paralegals use Letterhome to mail documents on behalf of clients who are outside Canada. If you're handling volume, see our professionals page for discount codes.
