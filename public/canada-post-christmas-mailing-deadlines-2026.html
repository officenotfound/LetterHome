Canada Post deadlines for cards and letters

Canada Post publishes a last day to send for each destination. The dates below are for cards and letters and come from its holiday sending page. They are between major urban centres, so they depend on where the mail starts and ends. Canada Post has a delivery standards tool for exact times between two postal codes.

Letters to Santa

For a reply from the North Pole through Canada Post's free program, the letter has to be sent by December 9. We cover how that program works, and the alternative of a letter you order for a child, in our guide to letters from Santa in Canada.

If you are sending from the United States

USPS publishes its own dates. A First-Class Mail card inside the contiguous US should go by December 17, and international service to Canada has a last day of December 9 for First-Class Package International and Priority Mail International. See our USPS Christmas deadlines for the full list.

If you are past the dates

Our own order-by dates are earlier than Canada Post's last day, because we print the letter and mail it within two business days. We suggest ordering by December 5 for a letter going to an address in Canada and by November 20 for international destinations. Those are estimates, and Canada Post delivery times are not guaranteed in December. A letter ordered later may still arrive in time, but we would not count on it. Details are on our holiday letter page.

Common questions

What is the last day to mail Christmas cards in Canada in 2026?

Canada Post lists December 22 for local cards, December 21 for regional cards and December 18 for cards going across the country. The dates depend on where the card starts and ends.

What is the last day to mail a Christmas card from Canada to the US?

Canada Post lists December 11 for cards going to the United States.

When do I need to mail a card to the UK or Europe from Canada?

Canada Post lists December 11 for the United Kingdom, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Switzerland and Japan, and December 9 for the rest of Europe, Australia, New Zealand and most of Asia.

What is the deadline for letters to Santa in Canada?

Canada Post says to send letters to Santa by December 9 to receive a reply from the North Pole.

Can I still mail a card after the last day?

You can, but Canada Post does not promise delivery before Christmas for mail sent after these dates. Mailing earlier is safer.
