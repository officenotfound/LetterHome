How to send a fax online
in Canada.

No fax machine and no phone line. Upload the document and the number, and it is sent for you.

Send a fax →

To send a fax online in Canada, upload your document as a PDF, JPG or PNG, enter the fax number with its area code, and pay. With Letterhome it costs $3 for up to 10 pages. It usually goes out within a few hours, and you get an email with the delivery time once the receiving machine has every page.

How it works

  1. Get the document ready

    Save it as a PDF, or take a clear photo of each page. A phone's scan feature works well.

  2. Enter the number and pay

    Type the fax number with its area code, add a cover page if you want one, and see every page before you pay.

  3. Get the confirmation

    We email you when the receiving fax machine has every page, with the date and time.

Check the number first: use the fax number from the organization's own website or letter. Many offices list a phone number and a fax number side by side, and a fax sent to a phone line will not go through.

Who still asks for a fax

Many doctors' offices, pharmacies, insurers and law offices, and some government programs, still ask for documents by fax. If a form tells you to fax it and you do not have a machine, an online service is the simplest way.

What you need

The document as a PDF, or a clear photo of each page as a JPG or PNG, up to 20 MB. The fax number, including the area code. An email address for your receipt and the delivery confirmation.

What it costs

$3 CAD for up to 10 pages, including a cover page if you add one, and $0.50 for each page after that, up to 50 pages. The exact price is shown before you pay. Faxes can go to Canadian and US numbers; toll-free numbers are not accepted.

If it does not go through

If the line is busy or does not answer, we try twice more. If it still does not go through, we email you about what happens next.

Is it private?

Your file is stored privately and deleted 24 hours after the fax is finished. A fax travels over the phone network without encryption, so for very sensitive documents, make sure the office is expecting it.

Common questions

How much does it cost to send a fax in Canada?

With Letterhome, $3 for up to 10 pages and $0.50 for each extra page. The price is shown before you pay.

Can I send a fax without a fax machine?

Yes. Upload the document online and it is sent to the fax number for you. You do not need a machine or a phone line.

How do I know my fax went through?

We email you once the receiving fax machine has every page, with the date and time. Keep that email as your record.

Can I send a fax to the US from Canada?

Yes. Canadian and US fax numbers both work, at the same price.

How long does it take?

Your fax usually goes out within a few hours. Sending itself takes a few minutes.
