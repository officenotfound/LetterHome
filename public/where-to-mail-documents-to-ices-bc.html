Where to mail documents to ICES British Columbia

Documents for an International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES) assessment at BCIT go to International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES), 3700 Willingdon Avenue, Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada, V5G 3H2. Immigration (ECA) applicants use the same street address marked c/o ECA, as published on BCIT's official ICES contact page.

Updated October 2026

If you're having your foreign credentials evaluated through BCIT's ICES program, whether for employment, education, or an immigration Educational Credential Assessment, and your institution can only issue paper transcripts, those sealed documents need to reach this Burnaby address by mail.

Source: bcit.ca — Contact ICES

Mark your envelope for ECA if it's for immigration: if your evaluation is for an Educational Credential Assessment used in an immigration application, address the envelope to “International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES) c/o ECA” so it's routed correctly.

ICES says it cannot be responsible for lost or misdirected mail, and recommends documents be sent directly by your educational institution wherever that's possible.
