Where to mail documents to UBC admissions

Mailed transcripts and application documents for UBC go to Enrolment Services at 2016 – 1874 East Mall, Vancouver, BC, V6T 1Z1 — the single address UBC publishes for mailed undergraduate documents, used even for Okanagan-campus applications.

Updated October 2026

If you're applying to UBC from outside Canada and your school can only issue a paper transcript, you'll need to get that sealed document into Canadian mail addressed to UBC's Vancouver campus, regardless of which UBC campus you're actually applying to.

Published on UBC's official undergraduate admissions contact page. UBC's own instructions say to mail to this Vancouver address even if your application is for the Okanagan campus.
Source: you.ubc.ca — Contact Us

Check your program first: some professional and graduate programs, including UBC's MD program, publish their own separate departmental mailing address. The Enrolment Services address above is for general undergraduate applications; your specific program's admissions page takes priority if it lists a different one.

UBC encourages electronic submission of transcripts and documents wherever your institution supports it. Mail is for when your school can only issue paper, or a program specifically requires a sealed hard copy.
