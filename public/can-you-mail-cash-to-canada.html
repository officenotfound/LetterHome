Can you mail cash to Canada?

Technically yes, but you shouldn't. Mailing cash to Canada isn't illegal, but cash in the mail is uninsured. If it's lost or stolen, there's no recourse. Better alternatives include bank transfers, money orders, or mailing a cheque. Cheques are legal, common, and fully allowed.

Can you mail a cheque to Canada?

Yes, mailing a cheque to Canada is perfectly legal and very common. A cheque is treated as a document, not as currency, so it passes through customs without restriction. If you need to send money to someone in Canada, a cheque is the safest option through the mail.

Why you shouldn't mail cash

Cash in the mail is risky for several reasons:

  • No insurance: Standard lettermail has no compensation if lost or stolen
  • No tracking: You can't prove it was received
  • Customs inspection: Mail containing cash may be held, inspected, or seized by Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA)
  • Declaration requirement: Bringing CAD $10,000 or more into Canada requires declaration, applies to mail as well

Better alternatives to mailing cash to Canada

  • Bank transfer / wire: Fast and traceable, most banks offer international transfers
  • Wise (formerly TransferWise): Low-fee international transfers to Canadian bank accounts
  • PayPal / Interac e-Transfer: For recipients with a Canadian bank account
  • Money order: Can be mailed, insured and replaceable if lost
  • Cheque: Legal, common, and safe to mail

Frequently asked questions

Can you mail cash to Canada?

Technically yes, but it's not recommended. Cash is uninsured in the mail and may be inspected by CBSA. Use a money order, cheque, or bank transfer instead.

Can you mail a cheque to Canada?

Yes. Cheques are documents and are fully allowed in international mail to Canada. They're the safest way to send money by post.

What documents can I mail to Canada?

Letters, legal documents, immigration forms, tax forms, contracts, certificates, photographs, and general correspondence are all permitted. Almost all standard documents are allowed.

Are there customs restrictions on letters mailed to Canada?

Standard documents and correspondence clear customs without issue. Restrictions apply to physical goods, regulated items, dangerous substances, and large amounts of currency.
