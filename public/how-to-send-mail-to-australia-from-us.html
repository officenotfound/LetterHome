Dates that matter in Australia

A letter timed for one of these days is the one that gets read twice. The order page shows the delivery time for their address before you pay, so you can plan the date.

  • Australia Day, 26 January.
  • Anzac Day, 25 April.
  • Mother's Day, the second Sunday of May.
  • Father's Day, the first Sunday of September.
  • Boxing Day, 26 December.

You can write a birthday or holiday letter ahead of time and use schedule for later so it lands on the day.

Words that change between the US and Australia

Your relatives will read it in their own English, so a few words land differently.

  • Mother: Mom in the US, Mum in Australia.
  • Postal code: ZIP code in the US, postcode in Australia.
  • Apartment: apartment in the US, unit or flat in Australia.
  • Where the letter lands: mailbox in the US, letterbox in Australia.
  • Time off: vacation in the US, holiday in Australia.
  • Biscuit: cookie in the US, biscuit in Australia.
  • Pavement: sidewalk in the US, footpath in Australia.

Dates trip people up too. In the US they are usually written month first, like 12/25/2026, while in Australia they are written day first, like 25/12/2026. Spell the month out, like 25 December 2026, and nobody has to guess.

The seasons are reversed. When it is summer in the US, it is winter in Australia, so a letter about the weather lands as a little window into another world.
