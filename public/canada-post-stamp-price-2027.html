Postage news

The Canadian stamp is going to $2.
Here is what is confirmed so far.

Budget 2026 puts the domestic stamp at $2 in 2027. The month, the booklet price and the U.S. and international rates are still not set.

Send a Letter →

The 2026 federal budget says a single domestic stamp will rise from $1.44 to $2 in 2027, then by about 7% a year after that. The budget does not say which month. Canada Post says stamps to the U.S. and other countries will rise too, but has not said by how much.

How it works

  1. Domestic stamp: $1.44 now, $2 in 2027

    A single stamp for a standard letter costs $1.44 today, and a stamp from a booklet, coil or pane costs $1.24. The budget puts the single stamp at $2 in 2027.

  2. Then about 7% a year

    After the jump to $2, the budget plans increases of about 7% a year. At that pace a stamp would pass $2.10 in the following year, though the exact figures will come from Canada Post.

  3. U.S. and international stamps will rise too

    Canada Post has said stamps for letters to the United States and other countries will go up, and that the amount has not been decided. No date has been given.

Not confirmed yet: the month the new price starts, the booklet and coil price, and the U.S. and international rates. We will update this page when Canada Post publishes them.

How the price has moved

Canada Post raised the single domestic stamp from $1.00 to $1.05 in January 2019 and to $1.07 in January 2020. In May 2024 it went to $1.15, with booklet stamps at 99 cents (Canada Post announcement). In January 2025 it jumped about 25% to $1.44, and booklet stamps went to $1.24 (Canada Post announcement).

The 2027 price would be the second big step in three years. Between 2020 and 2024 the single stamp did not change at all.

Why the price is going up

The budget says Canada Post's model no longer works as letter volume falls, and that the new price would bring Canadian postage closer to other OECD countries. Canada Post delivered about two billion domestic letters in 2025, down from 5.5 billion in 2006, according to reporting by the Canadian Press.

The same reporting says the budget estimates the increase would cost the average household about $6 a year and a small business about $9 a year, and that Ottawa is providing $1.4 billion over two years to support Canada Post.

What it means if you are outside Canada

You cannot buy a Canadian stamp from abroad, so none of this changes what you pay at a post office near you. Letterhome prints your letter and mails it from inside Canada. Our current prices are on the send page.

Common questions

When will a Canadian stamp cost $2?

In 2027, according to the 2026 federal budget. The budget does not name a month, and Canada Post has not published a date yet.

How much is a stamp in Canada right now?

A single stamp is $1.44. A stamp bought in a booklet, coil or pane is $1.24. See our guide to the cost to mail a letter in Canada for the full picture.

Will stamps to the U.S. and other countries go up too?

Yes. Canada Post has said U.S. and international letter stamps will rise, but it has not set the amounts or dates.

Where can I check the current price?

Canada Post publishes its current letter prices on canadapost-postescanada.ca. We update this page when they change.
