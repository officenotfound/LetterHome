Dates that matter in the US

A letter timed for one of these days is the one that gets read twice. The order page shows the delivery time for their address before you pay, so you can plan the date.

  • Independence Day, 4 July.
  • Thanksgiving, the fourth Thursday of November.
  • Mother's Day, the second Sunday of May.
  • Father's Day, the third Sunday of June.
  • Veterans Day, 11 November.

You can write a birthday or holiday letter ahead of time and use schedule for later so it lands on the day.

Words that change between the UK and the US

Your relatives will read it in their own English, so a few words land differently.

  • Mother: Mum in the UK, Mom in the US.
  • Postal code: postcode in the UK, ZIP code in the US.
  • Apartment: flat in the UK, apartment in the US.
  • Where the letter lands: letterbox in the UK, mailbox in the US.
  • Time off: holiday in the UK, vacation in the US.
  • Biscuit: biscuit in the UK, cookie in the US.
  • Pavement: pavement in the UK, sidewalk in the US.

Dates trip people up too. In the UK they are usually written day first, like 25/12/2026, while in the US they are written month first, like 12/25/2026. Spell the month out, like 25 December 2026, and nobody has to guess.
