Important: IRCC processing times do not include the time your application spends in the mail. If you're sending paper documents from outside Canada, add 6–14 business days for international post, or 3–5 business days for tracked courier.

Do IRCC processing times include mailing time?

No. The clock starts when IRCC receives your complete application — not when you send it. This means your total wait time is:

Total wait = Mailing time + IRCC processing time + Return mailing time (if applicable)

For paper applications sent from abroad, this easily adds 2–4 weeks to your total wait.

Frequently asked questions

How long does IRCC take to process applications in 2026?

Processing times vary significantly by application type. Visitor visas take as little as 14 days online; family sponsorship can take 24 months. Express Entry permanent residence targets 6 months for 80% of applicants. See the table above for current estimates.

Do IRCC processing times include mailing time?

No — processing time only counts from receipt to decision. Add 6–14 business days for international mail from outside Canada, or 3–5 days for courier.

Should I mail my IRCC documents or apply online?

Apply online whenever possible — it's faster and you get confirmation of receipt. Paper mail is only required for certain applications (e.g., some sponsorships, applications that include original documents). Check the specific IRCC guide for your application type.
