IRCC processing times 2026

IRCC processing times in 2026 range from 72 hours (eTA) to 24 months (family sponsorship). Visitor visas take about 14 days online; Express Entry permanent residence targets 6 months. Processing time doesn't include mailing time, add that separately.

Mailing time is separate. IRCC processing begins when they receive your application, not when you send it. From outside Canada, add 6–14 business days for international post, or 3–5 days for tracked courier. See our IRCC mailing guide →

Do IRCC processing times include mailing time?

No. The clock starts when IRCC receives your complete application. Your total wait is:

Total wait = Mailing time → IRCC processing time → Return mailing (if applicable)

For paper applications sent from abroad, this adds 2–4 weeks to your total wait. Applying online is faster and gives you instant confirmation of receipt.

Frequently asked questions

How long does IRCC take to process applications in 2026?

Visitor visas: ~14 days (online). Study permits: 8–16 weeks. Work permits: 2–10 weeks depending on stream. Express Entry (PR): 6 months (target). Family sponsorship: up to 24 months. See the table above for a full breakdown.

Do IRCC processing times include mailing time?

No, the processing clock starts on receipt. Add 6–14 business days for international post from outside Canada, or 3–5 days for courier.

Should I mail my IRCC documents or apply online?

Apply online whenever possible, it's faster and you get confirmation of receipt. Paper mail is required for some sponsorships and applications that include original documents. Check the specific IRCC guide for your application type.
