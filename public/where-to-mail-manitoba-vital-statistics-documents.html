Where to mail Manitoba vital statistics documents

Mail your completed application to the Vital Statistics Branch, 254 Portage Avenue, Winnipeg, MB, R3C 0B6. This address, published on the official vitalstats.gov.mb.ca site, handles mailed birth, marriage, and death certificate requests for events registered in Manitoba.

Updated October 2026

Newcomers and Canadians abroad who need a Manitoba-issued certificate — for a passport renewal, a credential assessment, or a sponsorship application — can download the application form for their document type and mail it to the branch's Winnipeg office.

Source: vitalstats.gov.mb.ca — How to apply for a certificate

Manitoba events only: the Vital Statistics Branch can issue a certificate only if the birth, marriage, or death occurred and was registered in Manitoba. If your event happened elsewhere, you'll need that province's own vital statistics office instead.

Compare your options: the official fee schedule lists $30 CAD for each certificate or certified copy, including the search. Manitoba also offers online ordering, which the branch's own turnaround figures suggest is faster — mail remains useful when you need to attach supporting documents or can't use the online service.

Why this matters for applicants abroad

Manitoba's Vital Statistics Branch doesn't take mailed applications by email, so the completed form and payment have to arrive as physical mail at this Winnipeg address.
