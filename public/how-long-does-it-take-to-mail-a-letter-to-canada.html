How long does it take to mail a letter to Canada?

Mailing a letter to Canada from abroad takes 1–6 weeks depending on the country. From the US: 7–21 days. From the UK: 7–14 days. From Australia: 10–20 days. From Asia: 1–4 weeks. From Africa: 2–6 weeks. With Letterhome, your document is mailed from inside Canada and delivers in up to 2 weeks regardless of where you are.

How long does Canada Post take once it arrives in Canada?

Once your letter enters the Canadian postal network, Canada Post Lettermail typically delivers within 2–9 business days depending on the origin and destination within Canada. Urban centres are generally faster; remote addresses or territories take longer.

Total time: international post + Canadian delivery

Your total wait is the combination of both legs:

  • Outbound: your country → Canada border (1–6 weeks depending on origin)
  • Customs clearing (usually 1–3 days for letters)
  • Domestic: Canada Post delivery within Canada (2–9 business days)

For a letter from India, that's potentially 14 days + 3 days customs + 5 days domestic = 3–4 weeks total in a best case.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to mail a letter to Canada from the US?

USPS First-Class Mail International from the US to Canada typically takes 7–21 days. Priority Mail International is faster at 6–10 days. With Letterhome your letter mails domestically from inside Canada — up to 2 weeks regardless.

How long does it take to mail a letter to Canada from the UK?

Royal Mail International Standard takes 7–14 business days to Canada. International Tracked takes 5–7 business days. With Letterhome, no international leg at all.

How long does Canada Post take to deliver within Canada?

Canada Post Lettermail delivers within Canada in 2–9 business days. Express Post is 1–2 business days. Letterhome letters use standard Lettermail, so plan for up to 2 weeks.

Does mailing time count toward IRCC processing time?

No. IRCC's processing clock starts when they receive your complete application. Add your outbound mailing time on top — see the IRCC processing times guide for current wait times.
