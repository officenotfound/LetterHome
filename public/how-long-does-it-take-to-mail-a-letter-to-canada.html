How long does it take to mail a letter in Canada, or to Canada?

Within Canada, a letter takes about 2–9 business days by Canada Post Lettermail. Mailing to Canada from abroad takes 1–6 weeks depending on the country (US: 7–21 days, UK: 7–14 days, Australia: 10–20 days). With Letterhome, your document is mailed from inside Canada and arrives in up to 2 weeks wherever you are.

How long does a letter take to mail within Canada?

Canada Post Lettermail usually takes 2–9 business days between two Canadian addresses. Canada Post publishes a delivery standard for each pair of postal codes: letters between nearby addresses, such as within the same city or province (Ontario, Quebec and British Columbia included), sit at the faster end of that range, while cross-country letters and rural or remote addresses sit at the slower end. Xpresspost is faster at 1–2 business days.

For an estimate on your exact route, enter both postal codes in Canada Post's delivery standards tool. Letters mailed through Letterhome use standard Lettermail, so allow up to 2 weeks to be safe.

How long does Canada Post take once it arrives in Canada?

Once your letter enters the Canadian postal network, Canada Post Lettermail typically delivers within 2–9 business days depending on the origin and destination within Canada. Urban centres are generally faster; remote addresses or territories take longer.

Total time: international post + Canadian delivery

Your total wait is the combination of both legs:

  • Outbound: your country → Canada border (1–6 weeks depending on origin)
  • Customs clearing (usually 1–3 days for letters)
  • Domestic: Canada Post delivery within Canada (2–9 business days)

For a letter from India, that's potentially 14 days + 3 days customs + 5 days domestic = 3–4 weeks total in a best case.

Canada Post Lettermail delivers within Canada in 2–9 business days. Xpresspost is usually 1–2 business days. Letterhome letters use standard Lettermail, so plan for up to 2 weeks.

How long does a letter take to mail in Canada?

Canada Post Lettermail usually takes 2–9 business days within Canada: faster between nearby addresses, slower across the country or to remote areas. Xpresspost is usually 1–2 business days.

How long does a letter take to mail in Ontario?

Ontario follows the same Canada Post Lettermail standard. Letters between nearby Ontario addresses are typically at the fast end of the 2–9 business day range, and letters to or from other provinces or remote communities take longer. Use Canada Post's delivery standards tool with both postal codes for the exact estimate.

Does mailing time count toward IRCC processing time?
