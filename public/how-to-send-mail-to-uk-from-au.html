Addressing a letter to the UK

Mrs A. Smith
12 High Street
Bath
BA1 1AA
UNITED KINGDOM

UK postcodes mix letters and numbers, like BA1 1AA or SW1A 1AA. A full postcode helps the letter reach the right door. Finish with the country on its own line, in capitals.

Dates that matter in the UK

A letter timed for one of these days is the one that gets read twice. The order page shows the delivery time for their address before you pay, so you can plan the date.

  • Mothering Sunday, the fourth Sunday of Lent, in March or April.
  • Father's Day, the third Sunday of June.
  • Bonfire Night, 5 November.
  • Remembrance Sunday, the Sunday closest to 11 November.
  • Boxing Day, 26 December.

You can write a birthday or holiday letter ahead of time and use schedule for later so it lands on the day.

Words that change between Australia and the UK

Your relatives will read it in their own English, so a few words land differently.

  • Apartment: unit or flat in Australia, flat in the UK.
  • Pavement: footpath in Australia, pavement in the UK.

The seasons are reversed. When it is summer in Australia, it is winter in the UK, so a letter about the weather lands as a little window into another world.
