Common questions

How long does a landlord have to return a deposit?

This varies by province and by whether deductions are being claimed. Check your provincial tenancy board's current rule rather than assuming a fixed number of days.

What if my landlord ignores the letter?

Most provinces have a landlord-tenant board or tribunal where you can file a claim. Your mailed demand letter and its mailing date become part of that record.

Do I need to send this by mail instead of text or email?

A mailed letter creates a clearer, dated record than a text thread, which matters if this goes to a tribunal. It isn't always required, but it's the stronger record.
