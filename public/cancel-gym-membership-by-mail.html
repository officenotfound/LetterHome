Cancellation letters

Cancel a gym membership,
in writing

Many gyms and clubs only accept a cancellation on paper. A short, dated letter is the simplest way to show you asked.

Send a letter

Check your contract for the notice period and the address to send cancellations to, write a short letter with your name, member number and the date you want the membership to end, keep a copy, and watch your next card statement to confirm billing stopped.

How it works

  1. Read your contract first

    Look for the notice period (often 30 days), the address cancellations must go to, and any rule about the method. Some contracts say registered mail or in person only.

  2. Write the letter

    Include your full name, member number, home address, the club location, today's date, and the date the cancellation should take effect. Ask for a written confirmation.

  3. Mail it and keep a copy

    Send it in time to meet the notice period. Keep the letter and your dated confirmation email somewhere you can find them if billing continues.

Check the method: if your contract says cancellations must go by registered mail or be handed over in person, follow that. Letterhome does not offer registered mail, so use the post office for it.

Why paper helps

A phone call or an email to the front desk can be forgotten. A dated letter, plus the confirmation email we send when it is mailed, gives you a record of when you asked. If a charge appears after your cancellation date, you can point to it when you contact the club or your card company.

If you are away from home

Moved cities, working abroad, or travelling for months? You do not need to go back to the club. Type the letter online and we print and mail it from Canada.

Common questions

How much notice do I need to give?

It depends on your contract. Thirty days is common, but check yours. Count from the day the club receives the letter, not the day you write it.

Can I cancel by email instead?

Only if your contract allows it. If it names a mailing address, a letter is the safer choice.

Does Letterhome give me proof the club received it?

No. We do not offer tracking or a signature on delivery. Our dated confirmation emails show when your letter was mailed. For signed proof of delivery, use registered mail at a Canada Post office.

What if they keep charging me?

Contact the club with a copy of your letter. If it continues, ask your card company about disputing the charge.
