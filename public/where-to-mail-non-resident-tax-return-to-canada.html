Where to mail a non-resident tax return to the CRA

Non-resident individuals mail their T1 return to one of two CRA tax centres, Winnipeg or Sudbury, depending on your country of residence. Get the wrong one and your return is simply forwarded internally, which can add delay. Below are the two current addresses as published by the CRA, and who routes where.

Updated October 2026

If you moved abroad, or you've never lived in Canada but have Canadian-source income to report, the CRA still expects a paper return for certain non-resident filings (including Section 216 and Section 217 elections where paper filing applies). Unlike resident returns, which route by province, non-resident returns route primarily by country of residence, with a secondary routing for a handful of Ontario cities.

Winnipeg Tax Centre

Routes: residents of the USA, UK, France, the Netherlands, Denmark — plus Canada-based filers in Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, the territories, and the Ontario cities of Belleville, Hamilton, Kingston, Kitchener, Waterloo, London, Ottawa, Peterborough, St. Catharines, Thunder Bay, and Windsor

Sudbury Tax Centre

Routes: residents of any other country not listed above, plus Canada-based filers in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, and the Ontario cities of Barrie, Sudbury, and Toronto

Double-check before you mail: CRA addresses and routing rules change. Confirm the current address for your exact country and form on canada.ca's "Where to mail your paper T1 return" page before sending anything, this page is a convenience summary, not a substitute for the official source.
