How it works

  1. Keep a copy of the letter itself

    Whatever you upload to be mailed, save it. If a dispute ever comes down to what the letter actually said, having the exact file beats trying to reconstruct it from memory.

  2. Keep the emails with the mailing date

    Every Letterhome order gets a confirmation email with the order number and recipient, then a “mailed” email with the date your letter was handed to Canada Post. That's our record of when it left our hands. It isn't an official Canada Post receipt, and it doesn't show when it arrives.

  3. Keep the confirmation somewhere you'll find it

    A dedicated email folder or a single starred message works. The order stays in your account under order history too, so it isn't only in your inbox.

Not official proof of mailing: our emails are Letterhome's own records, not a Canada Post receipt or certificate. They don't prove what was received or that it arrived. For official proof of mailing or delivery, use Registered Mail or Xpresspost at a Canada Post office. Letterhome doesn't offer these services.

Why the mailing date matters, and its limits

For some deadline-driven mail, such as certain CRA filings and LTB notices, the date something was mailed can matter more than the date it's read. Each body sets its own rules about what proof it accepts, so check them before you rely on a date. Letterhome letters use metered postage and may not carry a dated postmark, and our emails aren't an official Canada Post record. If the rules call for proof of mailing, use Registered Mail or Xpresspost at a post office.

Common questions

Does a Letterhome confirmation email count as legal proof?

No, it isn't an official Canada Post proof of mailing. Our order confirmation and our “mailed” email record your order, the recipient address and the date we handed your letter to Canada Post. That's useful for your own records, but whether anyone accepts it depends on the body you're dealing with. If you need official proof of mailing or delivery, use Registered Mail or Xpresspost at a Canada Post office; Letterhome doesn't offer those services.

What if I need proof of delivery, not just proof of mailing?

That needs Registered Mail or Xpresspost with tracking, bought at a Canada Post office. Letterhome doesn't offer either. Our emails only record the date we handed your letter to Canada Post, not arrival.

Can I get a copy of an old order confirmation?

Yes, log into your account and check order history, or email support@letterhome.ca with your order number.
