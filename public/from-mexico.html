Postage from Mexico to Canada in 2026

Sending a standard letter from Mexico to Canada via Correos de México costs approximately MXN 30–60 (roughly US$1.50–3). Delivery typically takes 2–6 weeks and tracking on standard international letters is unreliable. DHL and FedEx are faster but can cost US$25–55 for a small envelope.

With Letterhome, your letter is printed and mailed from inside Canada for $10 CAD (approximately MXN 145). It travels as domestic Canadian lettermail, no international routing or customs, typically arriving within Canada in up to 2 weeks.
