    Type your letter or attach a PDF or Word file. Photos work too, with color printing.

Forms and paperwork

Some people also use us for forms that have to reach Canada on paper: CRA tax forms, non-resident tax returns, IRCC documents and student transcripts. We print and mail what you upload and don't check forms for you, so make sure they're complete and signed where needed.

What customers say

Common questions

Can I mail a letter within the U.S.?

Yes. Where our local printing is running, the letter is printed and mailed inside the U.S. Otherwise it is printed in Canada and mailed from there, which takes longer. The order page tells you which applies, with the price, before you pay.

Can I send letters from the U.S. to family in other countries?

Yes. We send letters to Canada, the US, the UK, Australia and most other countries. Pick your route above for the address format and price.

Is Letterhome part of a postal service?

No. Letterhome is an independent company. We print your letter and hand it to the mail service for delivery, and every address is verified before printing.

Do you charge in U.S. dollars?

Yes. The prices on this page are what you pay at checkout.

How long does it take?

We send it within two business days of the order. Delivery within Canada usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, and international delivery up to 4 weeks. Standard mail has no tracking, so we can't give an exact date.

Is it tracked or signed for?

No. Standard mail has no tracking, signature or proof of delivery. You can follow your order through our own steps on the track page, and see our proof of mailing guide for the options.

Can I send it anonymously?

Yes. Skip the return address when you order. The envelope then carries our return address instead.

How do I get in touch?

Email support@letterhome.ca or use our contact page.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes. Toggle to Spanish at the top of the page.
