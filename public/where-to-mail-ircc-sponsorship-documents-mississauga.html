Where to mail IRCC sponsorship correspondence & fee paperwork

Correspondence and right-of-permanent-residence fee paperwork tied to the Case Processing Centre in Mississauga is mailed to IRCC's Digitization Centre, which processes physical mail for several case types centrally in Nova Scotia. The Mississauga office itself isn't open to the public and doesn't receive mail directly, IRCC's own page is explicit that physical submissions go to its digitization centre instead.

Updated October 2026

Family-class sponsorship files, and related permanent residence paperwork processed through CPC-Mississauga, don't get mailed to a Mississauga street address. IRCC consolidated physical mail intake for many of these files into a single digitization operation based in Nova Scotia.
