Common questions

Can I send a letter between any of these countries?

Yes. Write it on our site and we print it and send it to any address in the destination. Where our local printing is running the letter is printed in that country, and otherwise it is printed in Canada and sent from there. The order page tells you which before you pay.

What currency do you charge in?

Each country page shows set prices in its own currency, and you pay in that currency at checkout.
