How much does it cost to mail a letter to Canada?

Updated June 2026

In 2026, a single Canada Post stamp for a standard domestic letter is $1.44 — or $1.24 each in a booklet. From the United States, a letter to Canada costs about US$1.70 (one USPS Global Forever stamp). Those figures are postage only. The real cost of getting a letter into someone's mailbox also includes the envelope, paper, printer ink, and the trip to the post office — and, if you're outside Canada, the hassle of mailing internationally in the first place.

When doing it yourself is cheaper — and when it isn't

We'll be straight with you: if you're already in Canada, own a printer with ink, and have paper, envelopes, and time for the post office, a single stamp is the cheaper way to send one letter. That's the honest answer.

A print-and-mail service is the better deal when:

  • You're outside Canada and want a letter posted from inside Canada — no flight, no favours.
  • You don't own a printer (or a colour one), so DIY means buying hardware or finding a print shop.
  • You'd rather not spend 30–60 minutes and a trip on a single letter.
  • You're sending documents, official paperwork, or several pages in full colour.
  • You want to send anonymously or without sharing a return address.
