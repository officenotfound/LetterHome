Can I mail a package to Canada?

Yes, you can mail a package to Canada from any country. Use your local postal service for economy shipping or a courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS) for tracked delivery. Packages over CAD $20 in value may be subject to Canadian customs duties and GST. Letters and documents have no duty threshold and clear customs without fees.

How to mail a package to Canada

  1. Pack your item securely in a box or padded envelope
  2. Address it correctly — see the Canadian address format guide
  3. Complete a customs declaration form (CN22 for items under CAD $400, CN23 for higher value) — your post office will provide this
  4. Declare the contents and value accurately — undervaluing is illegal and can result in seizure
  5. Choose your service: standard post (cheaper, slower) or courier (faster, tracked)
  6. Pay and keep your tracking number

Canadian customs — what you need to know

  • Duty-free threshold: Gifts under CAD $60 are duty-free (but must be clearly marked "gift"). Commercial goods under CAD $40 may enter duty-free.
  • GST/HST: Applies to imported goods above the threshold. Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) collects it.
  • Prohibited items: firearms, certain food products, counterfeit goods, hazardous materials. See the full CBSA prohibited items list.
  • Processing time: Most packages clear customs within 1–3 days; complex cases take longer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I mail a package to Canada from outside Canada?

Yes. Use your local postal service or couriers like DHL, FedEx, or UPS. Complete a customs declaration form and declare contents accurately.

What is the customs duty threshold for packages to Canada?

Gifts: duty-free under CAD $60. Commercial goods: may be duty-free under CAD $40. Above these thresholds, GST/HST and possible duties apply.

Does Letterhome mail packages?
