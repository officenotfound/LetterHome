Postage from Nepal to Canada in 2026

Sending a standard letter from Nepal to Canada via Nepal Post costs approximately NPR 120–250 (roughly US$0.90–1.90). International airmail to Canada typically takes 2–4 weeks with limited tracking on standard letters. EMS and courier services are faster but significantly more expensive.
