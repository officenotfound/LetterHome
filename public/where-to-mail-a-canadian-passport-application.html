Where to mail a Canadian passport application

There's no single address — it depends on where you're mailing from. Applicants inside Canada mail to one of two Service Canada addresses by province or territory. Applicants outside Canada and the US submit through their nearest Government of Canada office abroad, each of which publishes its own address.

Updated October 2026

This is one of the few mailing addresses that genuinely isn't one address. Canadians abroad often search for "where to mail my passport application" expecting a single P.O. Box, the way provincial vital statistics offices work — but Service Canada routes passport mail two different ways depending on where the applicant is located. Both routes are covered below.

Scenario 1: Mailing from inside Canada

Domestic mail-in

If you're physically in Canada, your completed application and supporting documents go to one of two addresses, split by province and territory. A separate courier address applies to every province and territory.

Payment by mail: credit card (Visa, Mastercard, or American Express), an embossed prepaid card, or a certified cheque or money order in Canadian funds for the exact amount. Government cheques are accepted, payable to the Receiver General for Canada. Personal cheques and cash aren't accepted by mail.

Scenario 2: Mailing from outside Canada and the US

Abroad

If you're outside Canada and the US, canada.ca directs you to submit your application by mail or in person at the nearest Government of Canada office — an embassy, high commission, or consulate. There is no single international mailing address: each office lists its own, and several offices route applications to a shared regional passport processing centre serving nearby countries. The stated goal for these applications is 20 business days to process, plus mailing time.

Find your office before mailing: search canada.ca for the Government of Canada office covering the country you're mailing from — it will list the correct submission address or courier instructions for that region. Mailing to the wrong office's address, or to a domestic Service Canada address, will delay your application.

Payment from abroad: canada.ca describes paying online and including the printed receipt with the mailed application, or paying in person where the local office allows it.

Why this page looks different

Most of our "where to mail" guides point to one published address, because that's how the agency works — one P.O. Box for every applicant. Passport applications aren't like that, so this page sets out both routes rather than guessing at a single address that doesn't exist.
