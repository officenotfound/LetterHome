What it costs

See price at checkout
One letter to any address in Australia, with printing, envelope and postage included.

We price in Canadian dollars (CAD). The NZD figure is an estimate, refreshed weekly and rounded up to the nearest 50 cents. The exact amount shows at checkout, and your bank may convert it at its own rate. The first 2 black and white pages are included. Colour printing is C$1 extra, and each extra page is C$0.45 (C$0.55 in colour).

How it works

  1. Write it online

    Type your letter or attach a PDF or Word file. Photos work too, with colour printing.

  2. Add their address

    Their address in Australia, set out as the name, the street, then the suburb in capitals with the state and four-digit postcode, and AUSTRALIA on the last line.

  3. We print it and post it

    A person checks every order, then it's printed, put in an envelope and posted. You'll get an email when it's on its way.

  4. It arrives in their letter box

    International post to Australia typically takes up to 4 weeks. Where our Australian printing is running it can be quicker, and the order page tells you before you pay.

Addressing a letter to Australia

Ms Jess Nguyen
14 Wattle Street
FITZROY VIC 3065
AUSTRALIA

Postcodes are four digits, and the states are NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and NT. Finish with the country on its own line, in capitals.

What to write

  • Christmas in the middle of summer, so a letter about snow makes a fun contrast.
  • A birthday letter timed to land on the day, using schedule for later.
  • Photos from home, printed in colour, for the fridge.

Skip the group chat and write something they can read at the table. Plan ahead for Christmas, because it is the busiest time of year for the post.

Good to know

  • Standard post has no tracking and may not carry a dated postmark, so it is not proof of posting. See our proof of mailing guide if you need proof.
  • Want it anonymous? Skip the return address when you order and the envelope carries ours instead.
  • We don't check what's in your letter, so read it over before you send.

Common questions

Can I post a letter from New Zealand to Australia?

Yes. Write it on our site, and we print it and send it to any address in Australia. You don't need a printer or stamps.

How much does it cost?

The price is shown on this page, with postage and printing included. Colour printing is C$1 extra, and each page after the first 2 is C$0.45 (C$0.55 in colour).

How long will it take?

International post to Australia typically takes up to 4 weeks. Where our Australian printing is running it can be quicker, and the order page tells you before you pay. Standard mail has no tracking, so we can't give an exact date.

Is it tracked or signed for?

No. Standard mail has no tracking, signature or proof of delivery. See our proof of mailing guide for the options.

Can I include photos?

Yes. Attach them and pick colour printing so they come out in colour.

Can I send it anonymously?

Yes. Skip the return address when you order. The envelope then carries our return address instead.
