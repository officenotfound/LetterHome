Plenty of Kiwis spend a year or two in Canada on a working holiday, in the snow towns and the big cities. A phone call is easy. A letter for mum and dad to read at the table is better. Here's how to send one home.

How it works

  1. Write it online

    Type it on our site, or attach a PDF. Photos print in colour if you pick colour.

  2. Add the address

    Street, suburb, city and the four-digit postcode, with NEW ZEALAND on the last line.

  3. We print and post it

    A person checks the order, then it's printed, put in an envelope and posted. We email you when it goes.

What it costs

A letter to New Zealand is printed in Canada and posted from there, so it's priced as international post, in Canadian dollars. The New Zealand page shows the current estimate in New Zealand dollars, and the order page shows the exact price before you pay.

Ideas for what to write

  • The first time you saw proper snow, and how many layers it took.
  • A photo of your flatmates, with names, so nobody has to ask on the next call.
  • A birthday letter timed to land on the day. Schedule it for later.
  • A thank-you to the whānau who helped you get over there.

Worth knowing

  • International post to New Zealand typically takes up to 4 weeks, so don't leave a birthday letter to the last minute.
  • Standard post has no tracking, so we can't give an exact arrival date.
  • For Christmas, order early. See our holiday order-by dates.

Common questions

Can I send a letter home to New Zealand from Canada?

Yes. Write it on our site and we print it in Canada and post it to any New Zealand address.

How long will it take?

International post typically takes up to 4 weeks. Standard post has no tracking, so we can't give an exact date.

Can I include photos?

Yes. Attach them and pick colour printing so they come out in colour.

Do I need a New Zealand stamp?

No. Postage is included in the price.
