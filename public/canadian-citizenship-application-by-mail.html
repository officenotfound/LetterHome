How it works

  1. Confirm you meet the presence requirement

    Use IRCC's physical presence calculator to confirm your days before spending time on the rest of the package.

  2. Build the CIT 0002 package

    Include the application form, proof of status, language test results if required, and the current application fee receipt. IRCC's document checklist for your situation lists everything needed.

  3. Upload it at Letterhome

    Go to /send, enter the current IRCC citizenship processing address as the recipient, and attach your complete PDF package.

  4. We mail it the next business day

    Your application is mailed via Canada Post with a valid Canadian mailing date and a confirmation email as your record.

Missing documents are the most common delay. Use IRCC's document checklist for your specific situation before mailing, an incomplete package is often returned rather than processed.

Common questions

How many days of physical presence do I need?

Most adult applicants need 1,095 days within the five years immediately before applying. Some time outside Canada can still count under specific rules, check IRCC's calculator for your exact number.

Can I apply from outside Canada?

The application is mailed to a Canadian processing address regardless of where you are when you submit it, which is exactly the situation Letterhome is built for.

How long does processing take?

Check current IRCC processing times, citizenship applications can take a significant number of months.

How much does it cost to mail?

$10 CAD for the mailing itself. IRCC's application and right of citizenship fees are separate and paid directly to IRCC.
