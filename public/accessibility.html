Accessibility

Last updated: November 2026

We want everyone to be able to order a letter, whether they use a keyboard, a screen reader, a magnifier or a phone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. We have not had an independent audit yet, so some pages may fall short.

What we do

  • Every page has a skip link, a clear heading order and labelled forms, so keyboard and screen reader users can move around and fill in an order.
  • Everything you can click or tap can be reached and used with a keyboard, and the item you are on shows a visible outline.
  • We are bringing text and button colours up to the minimum contrast ratios in WCAG 2.1 AA, and the site works when you enlarge text or zoom.
  • The site follows your device's light or dark setting and your reduced motion setting.
  • It works at phone, tablet and desktop widths without sideways scrolling.
  • The decorative stamps and envelope are hidden from screen readers.

What is not finished

  • We have not had a third-party accessibility audit, and we have not tested with every assistive technology.
  • Some older guides may have headings or links that need work.
  • Card payment happens on a page run by Stripe, and we do not control its accessibility.
  • We do not check the files you upload. A letter is printed on paper exactly as you send it.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site is hard to use, or you need help placing an order another way, email support@letterhome.ca with the page address and what happened. Replies usually come within one business day, and we will tell you what we can fix and when.

Standards we follow

We build to WCAG 2.1 level AA and work toward the accessibility rules that apply to a Canadian business. We update this page as we improve the site.
