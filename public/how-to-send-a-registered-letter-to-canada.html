How to send a registered letter to Canada

To send a registered letter to Canada from abroad: take your letter to your local post office and request international registered mail to Canada. You'll receive a tracking number and proof of posting. Expect to pay US$10–25 depending on your country, significantly more than standard post.

When do you actually need registered mail?

Registered mail makes sense when:

  • You need proof of delivery, e.g. legal documents, IRCC appeals, court notices
  • You need a signature on delivery, required for some official processes
  • The document is irreplaceable, original certificates, passports (note: passports should use courier, not post)
  • You need to prove you sent it by a deadline

For most immigration documents, tax forms, and letters, standard post or Letterhome is sufficient. IRCC and CRA don't require registered mail unless specified.

How Letterhome compares

Letterhome isn't a registered mail service, we mail domestically within Canada via Canada Post Lettermail. However, because we mail from inside Canada, you bypass the expensive and slow international registered mail process entirely.

You get an order confirmation, then an email with the date your letter was handed to Canada Post. It's a record that you sent it, not an official proof of mailing. For most IRCC and CRA submissions, standard mail is fine unless registered mail is specified.

If you specifically need a signature on delivery or proof of delivery, use Canada Post Registered Mail or Xpresspost at a post office. Letterhome doesn't currently offer registered, tracked or signature mail.

How to send registered mail to Canada, step by step

  1. Write and seal your letter with the correct Canadian address format
  2. Visit your local post office
  3. Ask for "registered mail" or "international tracked mail" to Canada
  4. Pay the fee and receive your tracking number, keep it safe
  5. Track at your postal service's website; once in Canada, it may transfer to Canada Post tracking

Frequently asked questions

What is registered mail to Canada?

Registered mail provides a tracking number, proof of posting, and signature on delivery. It's more expensive than standard international post but gives you a verifiable record.

How much does registered mail to Canada cost?

From the US: US$15–20 (USPS Registered Mail) or US$35–60 (Priority Mail International). From the UK: £7–15 (Royal Mail International Tracked). From most countries, couriers like DHL cost US$30–80.

Does Canada Post deliver registered mail?

Yes. Canada Post delivers international registered mail once it enters Canada. Domestically, Canada Post Registered Mail costs from $8.95 and requires a recipient signature.

Do IRCC and CRA accept registered mail?

Yes, both accept registered mail and it's a good idea for important submissions. See the IRCC mailing guide and CRA tax forms guide for correct addresses.
