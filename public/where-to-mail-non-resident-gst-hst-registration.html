Where to mail a non-resident GST/HST registration

Non-resident businesses registering for a GST/HST account by paper mail Form RC1 to the CRA's Non-Resident Registration and Security unit at the Atlantic Tax Centre in Summerside, Prince Edward Island. This is a different office than the one that handles income tax returns, it's the unit that specifically processes non-resident business registrations and security (bond) requirements.

Updated October 2026

If your business is based outside Canada but you're required (or choose) to register for GST/HST, for example because you're carrying on business in Canada or providing digital services to Canadian customers above the relevant threshold, you can register by completing the GST/HST sections of Form RC1 and mailing it in.
