Common questions

Can you fill in the notice for me?

Not today. You attach the completed notice and we print and mail it. A guided service is only an idea so far, and it would need a qualified legal review first.

Is a mailed notice valid service?

That depends on the notice, the province and the facts, and we cannot advise on it. Check with your provincial tenancy board or a licensed paralegal or lawyer.

Do you give proof of mailing?

Regular lettermail has no tracking and may not carry a dated postmark. See our proof of mailing guide for what that means and what the options are.

How much does it cost?

$6 CAD for a letter within Canada, including the first 2 black and white pages, envelope and postage. Extra pages are $0.45 each.

Can I mail a notice to several tenants?

Each recipient is a separate order. For a building, email sales@letterhome.ca and we will quote it.
