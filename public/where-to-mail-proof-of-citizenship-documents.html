Where to mail proof-of-citizenship documents to IRCC

Proof of citizenship applications and citizenship search requests use separate PO Boxes at the same Sydney, Nova Scotia processing centre as citizenship grants. If you already hold Canadian citizenship and need a certificate as proof (for example to replace a lost one, or because you were never issued one), this is where the paperwork goes.

Updated October 2026

This is a common situation for people born to a Canadian parent outside Canada, or long-time citizens who never obtained a physical certificate. It's distinct from applying for citizenship, you're asking IRCC to confirm and document citizenship you already hold.

Documents IRCC has specifically requested from you
CPC-Sydney
P.O Box 12,000
Sydney, NS  B1P 7C2
Canada
If CPC-Sydney has written to you asking for additional documents on an existing file, this is the box for sending exactly those requested documents back.
Source: canada.ca — Case Processing Centre: Sydney, Nova Scotia

Notice these three boxes share the B1P 7C1/7C2 postal code pattern but are different box numbers, check the number carefully when addressing your envelope, it's how IRCC's Sydney centre sorts incoming mail to the right queue.
