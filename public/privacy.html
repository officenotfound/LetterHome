Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Letterhome is a small, individually-operated mail service. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the service, how we use it, and when we delete it.

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your email address (for order confirmation and support)
  • The sender and recipient postal addresses you provide
  • The content of your letter and any attached documents
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details
  • The IP address of the device used to place the order, for fraud prevention and to detect abuse

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to fulfil your order:

  • The letter content and addresses are used to print and mail your letter
  • Your email is used to send an order confirmation and to contact you if there is an issue with your order
  • Your IP address is stored with the order record to help us identify fraudulent or duplicate orders. It is not shared with anyone outside Letterhome.
  • We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose

Data retention

We delete the content of your letter and any attached documents within 7 days of mailing. We retain a basic order record — date, amount, and your email address — as required by Canadian tax and accounting regulations. We do not retain the text of your letter or recipient details beyond the 7-day window.

Third-party services

We use Stripe to process payments. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy. We use Canada Post to deliver physical mail. Beyond these, no third parties have access to your order information.

Letter content review

Each order is briefly reviewed by a human operator before printing to screen for prohibited content (threats, scams, or illegal material). This review is limited in scope and is not used for any other purpose.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at hello@letterhome.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@letterhome.ca or use the contact form.
