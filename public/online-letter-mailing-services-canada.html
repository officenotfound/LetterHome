What each one is good at

Online-Post

The lowest price for a single page, and the most developed account features: teams, prepaid credit with discounts and bulk mailing in large volumes. It sells Registered Mail and Express with tracking, and it publishes free tools such as a postage calculator. If you want registered mail from your desk, or you send a lot, it is a strong option.

PostPal

The same $6 starting price as Letterhome with a cheaper extra page ($0.35 against our $0.55), no account and registered mail at $31 all in. It also publishes a large library of letter templates and a directory of mailing addresses.

Letterhome

One flat price with the envelope and postage included, no account, and a person looks over every order and the address is checked before anything is printed. You can send a letter on a date you pick at no extra cost, and the same service covers the United States, the UK and Australia. We also do business mail by quote. What we do not do: registered mail, Xpresspost or tracking, and we cannot promise a resend or a refund once a letter has been mailed.

The post office counter

The right choice when you need proof of delivery from Canada Post itself, or when you are in Canada, have the letter printed and only need a stamp.

How to choose

  • You are sending one page and price matters most: Online-Post lists the lowest price.
  • You need Registered Mail without going to the post office: Online-Post or PostPal.
  • You want a flat price, nothing to set up and someone checking the address: Letterhome.
  • You are sending a long letter, many pages: compare the per-page price, Online-Post is the lowest per sheet.
  • You are already in Canada with a printer and one letter: a stamp.

Questions to ask any mailing service

  • Where is the letter printed and posted? A letter posted from inside Canada travels as domestic mail. One posted from abroad takes international delivery times.
  • Is the address checked before printing? A letter sent to a bad address is usually not refunded once it has been mailed.
  • What does it say about tracking? Standard mail has none. If a service shows a tracking number, find out which Canada Post product it is.
  • How long does it keep your letter? Look for a stated retention period in the privacy policy. Online-Post says it deletes files after 30 days, and PostPal says 30 days after mailing.
  • Who is behind it? A registered business name, a contact address and a support email are the minimum. Letterhome is a registered Canadian business, BN 787294073.
  • What is the total at checkout? Compare the final amount, not the starting price.
