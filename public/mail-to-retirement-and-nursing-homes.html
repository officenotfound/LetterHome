A letter means more

Family in a retirement or long-term care home often get less mail than they used to, and read it more carefully because of it. Here's how to address it correctly and send one from anywhere.

Send a Letter →

Include the facility's full name, your person's room or unit number, and the facility's mailing address exactly as the front desk gives it. Missing the room number is the most common reason mail gets delayed inside a large facility.

How it works

  1. Get the exact mailing format from the facility

    Call the front desk or check the facility's website. Many care homes want the resident's name, then room or unit number, then the facility name and address, in a specific order.

  2. Write your letter

    Type it, or attach a scanned handwritten letter or a printed photo alongside it, up to 5 files per order.

  3. Send it

    Go to /send, enter the facility's address with your person's name and room number, and we print and mail it from Canada within one business day.

Confirm the room or unit number. Large facilities sort internal mail by unit, a missing or outdated room number is the most common reason a letter takes longer to reach someone inside.

Why it's worth the extra step

Isolation is a real issue in long-term care, and a physical letter someone can hold, reread, and show to a nurse or a neighbour lands differently than a phone call. If they're hard of hearing or don't do video calls well, this is often the better way to reach them regularly.

Common questions

What if I don't know their exact room number?

Call the facility's front desk, they can confirm the current room or unit number and the correct mailing format before you send anything.

Can I send a printed photo along with the letter?

Yes, attach it as an image file, up to 5 attachments per order.

Can I schedule it to arrive on a specific date, like a birthday?

Yes, add the schedule option at checkout and choose the date you want it mailed to arrive around. See our scheduling guide.

How much does it cost?

$10 CAD to a Canadian facility, $20 CAD internationally.
