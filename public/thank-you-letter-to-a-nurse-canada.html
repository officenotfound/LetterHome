Letter writing

Thank-you letter
to a nurse.

Nurses rarely hear how things turned out. A short letter tells them.

Send a Letter →

Say who you are and when you were cared for, name the nurse if you can, describe one thing they did, and say how you are now. Address it to the nurse and unit at the hospital. National Nursing Week in Canada ran May 11 to 17 in 2026 and runs May 10 to 16 in 2027.

How it works

  1. Say when you were there

    Roughly when you or your family member were on the unit.

  2. Name the nurse

    Or describe them, such as "the night nurse on 4 West".

  3. Say how things are now

    Nurses often never find out how their patients did.

Make sure it arrives: address it to the nurse and the unit at the hospital's mailing address, and ask the hospital's patient relations office if you are unsure.

Example

Dear Nurse Okafor and the 4 West team,

My mother, Ruth Klein, was on your floor after her hip surgery in August. You noticed she wasn't eating and found the soup she liked. It was a small thing that made her feel cared for.

She is walking with a cane now and back in her garden.

Thank you,
Ellen Klein

When to send it

Any time is right. National Nursing Week includes International Nurses Day on May 12, Florence Nightingale's birthday. The Canadian Nurses Association lists the week as May 11 to 17 in 2026 and May 10 to 16 in 2027.

Common questions

When is National Nursing Week in Canada?

The week that includes May 12. The Canadian Nurses Association lists May 11 to 17 in 2026 and May 10 to 16 in 2027.

How do I send a thank-you letter to a nurse at a hospital?

Address it to the nurse and unit at the hospital's mailing address, or ask the patient relations office to pass it on.

What if I do not know the nurse's name?

Address it to the unit, describe the nurse and the date, and ask for it to be passed on.
