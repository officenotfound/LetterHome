How to send mail to Canada without a Canadian address

You do not need a Canadian address to send mail to Canada. You only need the recipient's Canadian address. Letterhome lets you send from any country — upload your document, enter the Canadian destination, and we post it from inside Canada.

You only need the recipient's address — not your own. A lot of people assume that to mail something to Canada they need a Canadian return address, a Canadian bank card, or somebody on the ground who can drop it off for them. None of that is true. All you need is the address you're mailing to, and a way to pay online.

With Letterhome, you submit your letter or document from wherever you are. Common situations

People order without a Canadian address for all sorts of reasons:

Living abroad, mailing to family. Whether you're in the US, UK, Australia, or anywhere else, you can mail to a Canadian relative without needing a Canadian address of your own.

Mailing government documents. IRCC, CRA, courts, tribunals, provincial offices — many government processes still require physical mail. If you're outside Canada, this is how you send it without getting on a plane.

No printer, no stamps, no problem. Even if you're in Canada but just don't have the supplies, the same service applies. You order online and it's handled.

Sending a surprise. No return address means the recipient doesn't know who sent it before they open the envelope — handy for cards, announcements, or anything where the reveal is part of it.
