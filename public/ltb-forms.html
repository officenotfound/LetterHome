How to serve an LTB notice by mail

  1. Download and complete the correct form

    Get the form from tribunalsontario.ca. Fill in all required fields including the termination date — remember to add at least 5 extra days for mail service.

  2. Calculate your termination date correctly

    Mail service adds 5 days to your deemed service date. For an N4 mailed August 7: deemed served August 12, 14-day notice ends August 26. That is your earliest valid termination date.

  3. Upload at Letterhome

    Go to /send, enter your tenant's address at the rental unit, and attach your completed form as a PDF.

  4. We mail it the next business day

    Your form is printed and mailed from Canada via Canada Post. Your confirmation email with the mailing date is your proof of service record for any LTB application.

The 5-day mail service rule

Under the Ontario Residential Tenancies Act, when a notice is served by mail, the law deems it served 5 days after it is put in the mail — regardless of when Canada Post actually delivers it. This means you must add 5 days before starting your notice period countdown.

If you get this wrong and your termination date is too early, the LTB may dismiss your application and require you to start over. Double-check your date before uploading.

Common questions

Does the LTB accept notices served by mail?

Yes. The Ontario Residential Tenancies Act explicitly permits service of notices by mail to the rental unit address. Mail is one of several accepted methods alongside personal service, courier, and email (with prior consent).

Do I need to send the notice from a Canadian address?

The notice needs to be mailed inside Canada via Canada Post so it carries a valid Canadian postmark. Your physical location as the landlord does not matter — you just need the letter to enter the Canadian postal system. Letterhome handles that part.

What proof of service do I have?

Your Letterhome order confirmation email includes the order date and mailing date. Keep this email. When filing an L1 or other application with the LTB, you will need to declare how and when you served the notice — the confirmation email establishes the mailing date.
