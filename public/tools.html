Free mailing tools for Canada

Updated November 2026

Three free tools for sending mail in Canada. They run in your browser, so nothing you type is sent to us, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Postage calculatorWork out the Canada Post letter rate from the size and weight of your envelope. Pages per stampSee how many sheets fit under the 30 g and 50 g limits for your paper and envelope. Address formatterBuild a Canadian address the way Canada Post prefers, and check the postal code.

The rates come from Canada Post's published Lettermail prices, checked November 2026. They cover domestic letters. Always confirm on Canada Post's site before you buy postage for something close to a weight or size limit.
