How to send a fax to Canada
from another country.

Living abroad and asked to fax something home? You do not need a fax machine or an international line.

Send a fax →

To fax a document to Canada from another country, upload it as a PDF, JPG or PNG to an online fax service and enter the Canadian fax number with its area code. With Letterhome it costs $3 CAD for up to 10 pages, you can pay with a card from any country, and the fax is sent over a North American line, so there is no international call.

How it works

  1. Scan or photograph the document

    A PDF is best. A clear photo of each page also works.

  2. Enter the Canadian fax number

    Ten digits with the area code, such as 613-555-0123. You do not need to add a country code.

  3. Pay and get the confirmation

    Pay by card from any country. We email you when the fax has been delivered.

Writing the number: a Canadian fax number has 10 digits, starting with the area code. If an office gives it as +1 613 555 0123, the +1 is the country code for Canada and the US, and you can leave it out.

Why fax from abroad

Canadians living abroad are often asked to fax documents home: to a doctor or pharmacy, an insurer, a bank, a lawyer or a former employer. A fax machine is rare outside offices, and calling a Canadian fax line from overseas can be costly or impossible.

What it costs

$3 CAD for up to 10 pages and $0.50 for each page after that, up to 50 pages. Your card is charged in Canadian dollars, so your bank may convert the amount. The exact price is shown before you pay.

When to mail instead

Some offices do not accept faxes, or need an original signature. If they want the document on paper, we can print it and mail it from inside Canada instead.

Common questions
