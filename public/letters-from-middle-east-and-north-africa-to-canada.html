How it works from the Middle East and North Africa

  1. Upload your letter or document (PDF, Word or image, up to 5 files) at letterhome.ca/send. Documents can be in any language; we print exactly what you upload.
  2. Enter the Canadian address of the recipient and pay online by card. No account is needed.
  3. We print and mail it in Canada within one business day. It is delivered by Canada Post as ordinary lettermail, so there is no international routing or customs step for the letter.

This suits family letters, immigration and tax paperwork, bank and university documents, and legal notices that must reach a Canadian address on paper. For tracked or signature delivery, registered mail is available as an add-on.
