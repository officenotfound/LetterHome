Civic letters

How to write a letter
to your MP in Canada

A short, specific letter from a constituent gets read. Here is the address and what to put in it.

Send a letter

Mail to any Member of Parliament can be sent postage-free inside Canada to the House of Commons, Ottawa, Ontario K1A 0A6. Put your own address and postal code on the letter so the office knows you are a constituent, keep to one issue, and say what you want them to do.

How it works

  1. Find your MP

    Look up your riding on the House of Commons website with your postal code. Write to the MP for the place where you live.

  2. Write one page on one issue

    Say who you are, what the issue is, what it means to you, and what you want your MP to do. Be polite and specific.

  3. Address it and send it

    Use the House of Commons address, with the MP's name, or the constituency office address on the MP's page.

Postage-free has limits: the House of Commons says mail to MPs is postage-free inside Canada. If you are mailing from another country, normal international postage applies.

The address

Name of MP, M.P.
House of Commons
Ottawa, Ontario
K1A 0A6

The House of Commons contact page confirms the postage-free rule. Constituency offices also have their own addresses, which are shown on each MP's page.

What to include

Your name, home address and postal code at the top, so the office knows you live in the riding. A clear first sentence on the issue. One or two reasons, ideally personal. A specific request: vote for or against, raise it in committee, or meet to discuss it. Thank them for reading it.

If you live abroad

Canadians abroad are still constituents of the riding where they last lived. Type your letter online, add the House of Commons address, and we print and mail it from inside Canada, so you do not need to arrange Canadian stamps.

Common questions

Do I need a stamp?

Not for mail to an MP at the House of Commons from within Canada, according to the House of Commons. Mail from outside Canada needs normal postage.

Is a letter better than an email?

Both are read. A letter on paper stands out and is harder to ignore.

How long until I hear back?

It varies by office and by how much mail they are getting. Say in your letter that you would like a reply.

Can I send the same letter to more than one MP?

Yes. You can write to your own MP, and to ministers or critics for the issue you care about. See our bulk mailing page for sending several.
