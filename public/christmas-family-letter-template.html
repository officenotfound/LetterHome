A simple structure

  1. Open warmly

    One line that says who it is from and that you are glad to be writing.

  2. One paragraph per person

    Two highlights each is plenty. Include the children.

  3. One story

    A single moment people will picture. It is the part they remember.

  4. One thing you are grateful for

    Keep it to a sentence.

  5. Look ahead and invite a reply

    A plan for next year and a request to hear back.

A template to copy

Dear family and friends, Happy holidays from all of us at [address or city]! Here is our year in one page. [Name]: [one or two highlights, such as a new job, a trip, a goal reached.] [Name]: [one or two highlights.] [Name]: [one or two highlights.] The moment we will remember: [one short story, ideally funny or kind.] Something we are grateful for: [one sentence.] Looking ahead: [one plan or hope for next year.] We think of you often, and we would love to hear how you are. Please write back or call. With love, [Names]

Tips

  • Keep it to one page. It will actually be read.
  • Be honest, not polished. A hard year, mentioned briefly, makes the good news feel real.
  • Add a photo. A family photo on a second page is the first thing people look for. Print in colour for a few dollars more.
  • Sign it by hand if you can. If you are sending a printed copy, a handwritten line at the bottom makes it personal.

Common questions

How long should a Christmas family letter be?

One page. People read a short letter to the end, and a long one tends to get set aside.

What should I include?

A highlight or two for each person, one short story, something you are grateful for, and a look at next year.

Can I add a photo?

Yes. Attach a PDF with the photo on a second page. Colour printing is $1 extra.

How do I mail it to relatives in Canada if I live abroad?

Write it on our site and we print it and mail it from Canada. Check the order-by dates so it arrives in time.

Can I send the same letter to many people?

Each recipient is a separate order. For a long list, email sales@letterhome.ca and see bulk mailing.
