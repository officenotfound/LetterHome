How it works

  1. Write it online

    Type your letter or attach a PDF or Word file. Photos work too, with colour printing.

  2. Add the UK address

    Use the full postcode. There is an example below.

  3. We print and post it from Canada

    A person checks every order, then it is printed, put in an envelope and posted. You get an email when it is on its way.

  4. It lands on the doormat

    Then put the kettle on. Delivery to the UK is typically up to 4 weeks.

Addressing a letter to the UK

Put each part on its own line, and finish with the postcode and the country.

Mrs A. Smith
12 High Street
Bath
BA1 1AA
United Kingdom

UK postcodes mix letters and numbers, such as SW1A 1AA or M1 1AE. A full postcode helps your letter get to the right door. If you are not sure of one, the Royal Mail Postcode Finder can look it up.

Post for Christmas

Letters to the UK typically take up to 4 weeks, and December is a busy month for the post. For Christmas, we suggest ordering by 20 November. That date is our estimate and not a guarantee. See the full holiday order-by dates, and use schedule for later to choose the day it is posted.

Can I send a letter home to the UK from Canada?

Yes. Write it on our site and we print it in Canada and post it to any UK address. You do not need a printer, stamps or a trip to the post office.

How long does it take to reach the UK?

Typically up to 4 weeks. Lettermail has no tracking, so we cannot give an exact arrival date. For Christmas, we suggest ordering by 20 November.

Can I send photos or a PDF?

Yes. You can attach up to 5 files. Choose colour printing if you want photos to print in colour.

Is it recorded or signed for?

No. Standard lettermail has no tracking, signature or proof of delivery. See our proof of mailing guide for the options.
