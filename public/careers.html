How we work

Letterhome prints and mails letters for people and businesses in Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom. We are a small team, so you will deal directly with the founder, and decisions are quick.

We never charge applicants for anything: no training fees, no starter kits and no equipment to buy from us. If anyone asks you to pay to work with Letterhome, it is not us. Please tell us at support@letterhome.ca.
