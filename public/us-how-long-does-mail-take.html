How long does mail take
in the US?

A First-Class letter takes 1 to 5 days. Here is what decides which end of that range yours lands on.

USPS aims to deliver First-Class Mail in 1 to 5 days. Letters within the same area often arrive in 1 to 2 days; letters across the country can take 4 or 5.

Updated November 2026

The short answer

USPS sets a "service standard" for First-Class Mail of 1 to 5 days. That is a target, not a promise. A birthday card mailed across town will usually land in a day or two. A letter from Maine to Arizona sits at the slow end, around 4 or 5 days.

Days are counted as delivery days. USPS delivers letters Monday through Saturday, so a letter mailed on a Friday afternoon is unlikely to arrive before Monday or Tuesday.

What decides the number of days

Three things matter most: the distance between the two processing plants, the time you drop the letter off, and whether it has to travel by truck or plane.

Mail is sorted at regional processing plants, not at your local post office. A letter collected after the day's last pickup waits until the next day before it moves at all. Long trips between plants are mostly done by truck now, which is part of why cross-country letters take longer than they did a decade ago.

What changed in 2025

In 2025 USPS changed how it moves mail between plants. It kept the 1 to 5 day range, but moved some routes within it. By its own count, about 75% of First-Class Mail kept the same standard, 14% got faster and 11% got slower. Some rural areas had a day added.

If you want the exact standard between two ZIP codes, USPS publishes a service standards lookup on usps.com.

Can you track a letter?

Not a stamped one. An ordinary First-Class letter has no tracking number. If you need proof that a letter was delivered, use Certified Mail at the post office counter. It costs more, but you get a receipt and delivery tracking.

Common questions

How long does a letter take to arrive in the US?

USPS aims to deliver First-Class Mail in 1 to 5 days. Letters within the same region often take 1 to 2 days, and letters across the country can take 4 or 5.

Does USPS deliver letters on Saturday?

Yes. USPS delivers letters Monday through Saturday. Sunday delivery is limited to some packages.

Can I track a letter sent with a stamp?

No. A stamped First-Class letter has no tracking. Certified Mail, bought at the post office counter, gives you a receipt and delivery tracking.
