How to write a letter
to the Prime Minister.

The Ottawa address, the right greeting, and how to make a letter count.

Send a Letter →

Write to the Office of the Prime Minister, 80 Wellington Street, Ottawa, ON K1A 0A2. Open with "Dear Prime Minister", keep to one issue, and include your name and full address so the office can reply. For a problem with a federal program, your own MP is often the faster route.

How it works

  1. Pick one issue

    Say in your first sentence what you are writing about and what you want the government to do.

  2. Add your details

    Your full name, address and postal code, so the office can reply and see where you live.

  3. Address it and mail it

    Use the address of the Prime Minister's Office in Ottawa, or the online form on pm.gc.ca if you prefer.

Your MP may be faster: for help with a federal program such as passports, immigration, EI or the CRA, write to your own MP. Their staff help constituents with these files every day.

The address

Office of the Prime Minister
80 Wellington Street
Ottawa, ON
K1A 0A2

This is the address the Prime Minister's Office gives for mail. It also takes messages through an online form on pm.gc.ca.

How to start and end it

Open with "Dear Prime Minister" and close with "Sincerely" or "Respectfully", then your signature and typed name. Keep it polite and to one page, and give a personal reason the issue matters to you.

Postage

Mail to members of the House of Commons is postage-free inside Canada under the Canada Post Corporation Act, and the Prime Minister is a member. Postage-free mail to MPs is usually sent to the House of Commons, Ottawa, Ontario K1A 0A6. If you write to the Prime Minister's Office on Wellington Street instead, a stamp is the safe choice.

Common questions

What is the address to write to the Prime Minister of Canada?

Office of the Prime Minister, 80 Wellington Street, Ottawa, ON K1A 0A2.

How do I start a letter to the Prime Minister?

"Dear Prime Minister" is the usual opening. Close with "Sincerely" or "Respectfully".

Do I need a stamp to write to the Prime Minister?

Mail to members of the House of Commons is postage-free inside Canada, and the Prime Minister is an MP. If you use the Wellington Street office address, add a stamp to be safe.

Will the Prime Minister reply?

The office receives a large amount of mail, and staff handle it. Include your full address if you want a reply.

Should I write to the Prime Minister or my MP?

For a personal problem with a federal program, your MP is usually faster. For a national issue, writing to both is common.
