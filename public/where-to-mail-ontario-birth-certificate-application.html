Where to mail an Ontario birth certificate application

Mail your completed birth certificate application to ServiceOntario, Office of the Registrar General, P.O. Box 4600, 189 Red River Road, Thunder Bay, Ontario, P7B 6L8. This is the exact address published on the official ontario.ca page for getting or replacing an Ontario birth certificate.

Updated October 2026

Newcomers and Canadians abroad often need an Ontario-issued birth certificate to apply for a passport, sponsor a family member, or complete a credential assessment — but the Office of the Registrar General only accepts paper applications by mail or in person, not plain email. If you don't have a Canadian return address to work from, that's where Letterhome can help send the completed form.

Use the right P.O. Box: the Office of the Registrar General uses different P.O. Box numbers for different services at the same Thunder Bay street address. A name change application, for example, goes to P.O. Box 3000, not P.O. Box 4600. Always use the box number printed on your specific application form.

Online ordering is also available: ServiceOntario offers online ordering by credit card for many applicants. Mail is the route for anyone who prefers paper, needs to attach supporting documents, or can't use the online service from outside Canada.

Why this matters for applicants abroad

A birth certificate request can't be emailed or faxed to the Office of the Registrar General — it has to travel as physical mail to a specific P.O. Box in Thunder Bay. For someone living outside Canada without a Canadian mailing address of their own, Letterhome prints the completed, signed application and mails it from inside Canada by Canada Post, so it arrives the same way a form mailed by a Canadian resident would.
