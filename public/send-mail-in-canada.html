How to send a standard letter, step by step

  1. Print and fold it. Three folds fit a #10 business envelope. Check the weight first if the letter is more than a few pages (pages per stamp).
  2. Address the envelope. Recipient, street address, then city, province code and postal code on one line. The address formatter builds it in the form Canada Post prefers, and this guide explains each part.
  3. Add a return address in the top left corner.
  4. Put on the right postage. A single $1.44 stamp covers a standard letter up to 30 g.
  5. Post it in a street mailbox, a community mailbox or at a post office.

What people most often mail in Canada

Most mail in Canada is still the ordinary stamped letter, but a few kinds of mail have their own rules and deadlines.

  • Government forms and applications. Many still go by mail. See which Canadian government forms still require mail, and the guides for the CRA and IRCC.
  • Legal and tenancy notices. These often depend on proof of delivery. See landlord notices and how to keep proof you sent a letter.
  • Cancellations and complaints to companies that ask for a written notice, such as a gym membership.
  • Personal letters and cards to family, a care home, a student or a child at camp.
  • Holiday mail. Dates matter near Christmas. See the 2026 Christmas mailing deadlines.

A quick checklist before you post

  • The postal code has six characters, in the form A1A 1A1, and matches the city and province.
  • The sealed envelope has been weighed, or counted against the pages-per-stamp chart.
  • The return address is in the top left corner, in case the letter comes back.
  • You have kept a copy of what you sent and the date you posted it.
  • If a deadline depends on arrival, you have chosen Registered Mail or Xpresspost and allowed extra days.

How long mail takes in Canada

Canada Post does not guarantee delivery times for standard Lettermail. In practice a letter takes 2 to 9 business days. Mail between nearby addresses is quicker, and mail across the country or to remote areas is slower. Allow up to two weeks if the date matters. Xpresspost, which adds tracking, is usually 1 to 2 business days. More detail is in how long a letter takes in Canada.

Tracking and proof of delivery

Standard Lettermail is not tracked, so there is no scan when it is posted or delivered. If you need to show that a letter arrived, for a legal notice or a government deadline for example, buy Registered Mail or Xpresspost at a Canada Post counter. The differences are in registered mail vs Xpresspost vs regular mail. We send standard Lettermail and cannot add either service, so for proof of delivery use the post office.

What not to mail

Canada Post advises against mailing cash, and anything over 500 g or larger than 380 by 270 by 20 mm is no longer Lettermail. See whether you can mail cash in Canada and letters versus packages.

Sending mail to Canada from another country

International postage into Canada is slower and costs more than a domestic stamp. From the United States, a letter to Canada takes about 7 to 21 days, and from the UK about 7 to 14 business days. A letter posted from inside Canada travels as domestic mail instead. The guides for each country cover the details: from the United States, from the UK, from Australia and from anywhere.
