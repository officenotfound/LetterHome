Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

All sales are final. Due to the nature of physical mail, we do not offer refunds once payment is received. We disclose this clearly before you pay. If something goes wrong, we will do our best to make it right.

Why there are no refunds

Once your payment is received, your order enters the production queue. The printing, paper, envelope, and postage costs are committed immediately. Standard letter mail does not have tracking — once a letter is deposited with Canada Post, it cannot be intercepted, recalled, or traced. This is the nature of the service and is disclosed before checkout. By placing an order, you acknowledge and accept this.

What we will do if your letter doesn't arrive

Standard letter mail is highly reliable. Over 99% of our letters arrive within the expected window. If yours doesn't, here's what happens:

  • Within Canada: If your letter hasn't arrived within 2 weeks of the order date, contact us. We'll confirm the details were correct and, where we're satisfied the issue wasn't caused by an incorrect address you provided, we'll resend at no charge.
  • International: If your letter hasn't arrived within 4 weeks, contact us. We take non-delivery seriously and will resend where appropriate.
  • Our printing error: If we printed or addressed your letter incorrectly due to a mistake on our part, we will always reprint and resend at no charge.

This is a goodwill commitment, not a contractual guarantee — but we stand behind our service.

Expected delivery times

For reference, standard letter mail delivery times from Canada are typically:

  • Within Canada: up to 2 weeks (most letters arrive within 1 week)
  • International: up to 4 weeks (most letters arrive within 2–3 weeks; some destinations may take longer)

These are estimates based on Canada Post's standard letter mail service. We do not offer tracked or registered mail by default — contact us if you need a tracked option for important documents.
