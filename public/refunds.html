Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

You can cancel for a full refund until your order is sent to the printer. Every order is reviewed by a person first, so email us quickly. Once it's sent to the printer it can't be stopped or refunded, but if your letter doesn't arrive, we'll reprint and resend it once at no charge (conditions below).

Cancellations and refunds

To cancel, email support@letterhome.ca with your order number. If your order hasn't been sent to the printer yet, we'll cancel it and refund it in full. If we decline an order at review, we refund it in full too. Once an order is sent to the printer, printing and postage are committed and it can't be stopped, cancelled or refunded. Once a letter is in Canada Post's hands, it can't be traced or recalled, that's how standard lettermail works. This is disclosed before checkout, and by placing an order you accept it.

What we'll do if your letter doesn't arrive

Canada Post's standard lettermail is reliable but untracked. If yours doesn't arrive within our estimated window (2 weeks within Canada, 4 weeks elsewhere), we'll reprint and resend it once at no charge when all of these apply:

  • You tell us in time: email us with your order number within 30 days (Canada) or 60 days (all other destinations) of the “mailed” email we send when your letter is handed to Canada Post.
  • The address was verified: the recipient address passed address verification when the letter was printed.
  • Not covered: addresses entered incorrectly by you, and anonymous letters (sent without a return address) that come back to Letterhome. Returned letters aren't re-mailed or refunded.

This is one free reprint per order, not a refund or a delivery guarantee. We extend it because the alternative, a customer paying for a letter that didn't arrive, isn't a position we want to leave anyone in. And if the mistake is ours, such as a printing error or an address we got wrong, we reprint and resend it free, no conditions.

Expected delivery times

Our estimates for standard Canada Post lettermail, counted from when your letter is handed to Canada Post:

  • Within Canada: up to 2 weeks (most letters arrive within a week; remote areas take longer)
  • United States and international: up to 4 weeks (most letters arrive in 1–3 weeks; some destinations take longer)

We don't currently offer tracked, registered or signature mail. Lettermail has no tracking; we email you when your letter is handed to Canada Post. If you need proof of delivery, use Registered Mail or Xpresspost at a Canada Post office.
