Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

You can cancel for a full refund until your order is sent to the printer. Email us as soon as you can. Once it's sent to the printer it can't be stopped or refunded.

Cancellations and refunds

To cancel, email support@letterhome.ca with your order number. If your order hasn't been sent to the printer yet, we'll cancel it and refund it in full. If we decline an order at review, we refund it in full too. Once an order is sent to the printer, printing and postage are committed and it can't be stopped, cancelled or refunded. Once a letter is in Canada Post's hands, it can't be traced or recalled, that's how standard lettermail works. This is disclosed before checkout, and by placing an order you accept it.

If your letter doesn't arrive

Canada Post's standard lettermail is reliable but untracked, so we can't offer a reprint, resend or refund for a letter that has been mailed. Our delivery estimates are 2 weeks within Canada and 4 weeks elsewhere.

If your letter hasn't arrived after the estimated time, or you think something went wrong on our end, email us with your order number and we'll review it. Letters that come back to us, including anonymous letters sent without a return address, are not re-mailed or refunded.

Expected delivery times

Our estimates for standard Canada Post lettermail, counted from when your letter is handed to Canada Post:

  • Within Canada: up to 2 weeks (most letters arrive within a week; remote areas take longer)
  • United States and international: up to 4 weeks (most letters arrive in 1–3 weeks; some destinations take longer)

We don't currently offer tracked, registered or signature mail. Lettermail has no tracking; we email you when your letter is handed to Canada Post. If you need proof of delivery, use Registered Mail or Xpresspost at a Canada Post office.
