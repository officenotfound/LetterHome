Letters to yourself

Write a letter
to your future self

Write it today, pick a date, and it arrives in your mailbox when you have forgotten what you wrote.

Send a letter

Write your letter online, choose the date it should be mailed (up to a year ahead), enter the address where you will be then, and we print and mail it on that day. The scheduling option costs extra and the price is shown before you pay.

How it works

  1. Decide the date

    An anniversary, a birthday, the end of a hard year, the day before a big exam. Pick a date within the next year.

  2. Write it

    Write about where you are now, what you are afraid of, what you hope for, and what you want to remember. Be specific. Include small details.

  3. Use an address that will still work

    If you might move, send it to a parent's or friend's address, or to a place you are sure to be.

Prompts to get started

What are you worried about today? Who are you spending the most time with? What song is stuck in your head? What do you want to have done by the time you read this? What would you tell yourself if you could? Describe an ordinary day so you can compare it later.

How scheduling works

You can pick any send date within a year. We mail the letter within two business days of that date and Canada Post delivery follows. Schedule a few days earlier if you need it to land on a particular day. See schedule a letter for later for the details.

For birthdays and milestones

Parents write letters to children to be read years later. The one-year limit means you cannot schedule a letter for a child's 18th birthday today, but you can order one each year, or write one now and mail it when the day comes. We can send it on the date you choose within the year.

Common questions

How far ahead can I schedule?

Up to one year from the day you order.

What happens if I move?

The letter goes to the address you entered. Use an address that will work on the date you chose, such as a family member's.

Can I send it to someone else instead?

Yes. The same option works for any recipient. A letter timed for a birthday or anniversary is a popular choice.

Is it private?

Your letter is printed for mailing and not published. See our privacy policy for how we handle your order.
