How much is postage for a large letter in Canada?

A large letter costs $2.61 to mail within Canada if it weighs up to 100 g (about 3.5 oz). The price rises in steps to $7.36 at 500 g. A standard-size letter costs $1.44 up to 30 g and $1.75 from 30 g to 50 g. Anything over 50 g, or bigger than 245 by 156 mm, mails at the large letter rates.

Updated November 2026

What counts as a large letter

Canada Post does not use the term "large letter". It sells two kinds of Lettermail: standard and other. Standard Lettermail is 50 g or less and no bigger than 245 by 156 mm and 5 mm thick. Other Lettermail covers heavier or bigger items, up to 380 by 270 by 20 mm and 500 g. This guide calls those large letters.

A few common envelopes show where the line falls:

  • A letter folded in three in a #10 business envelope (about 105 by 241 mm) is standard size.
  • A 6 by 9 inch envelope (about 152 by 229 mm) is still standard size, as long as it weighs 50 g or less and stays under 5 mm thick.
  • A 9 by 12 inch envelope (about 229 by 305 mm) is longer than 245 mm, so it mails as a large letter even when it is light.

Every item must also be at least 140 by 90 mm. Anything bigger than 380 by 270 by 20 mm, or heavier than 500 g, is no longer Lettermail and has to go by a parcel service.

Sending a letter to Canada from another country

These rates apply to a letter posted inside Canada. If you live abroad, a stamp from your own country is a different price, and the letter then travels internationally. Frequently asked questions

How much is postage for a 3 oz letter in Canada?

A 3 oz letter weighs about 85 g. That is over the 50 g limit for standard Lettermail, so it mails as other Lettermail, which is $2.61 for anything up to 100 g.

How much is postage for a 1.5 oz letter in Canada?

A 1.5 oz letter weighs about 43 g. If the envelope is standard size (up to 245 by 156 mm and 5 mm thick), the rate for 30 g to 50 g is $1.75.

How much does it cost to mail a 9 by 12 inch envelope in Canada?

A 9 by 12 inch envelope measures about 229 by 305 mm, which is longer than the 245 mm standard limit. It mails as other Lettermail, at $2.61 up to 100 g and $4.29 from 100 g to 200 g.

What is the biggest letter Canada Post will mail as Lettermail?

Other Lettermail goes up to 380 by 270 by 20 mm and 500 g. Anything bigger or heavier no longer qualifies as Lettermail and has to go by a parcel service.

Does the envelope count toward the weight?

Yes. Canada Post prices the whole item, so weigh the sealed envelope with everything inside it.
