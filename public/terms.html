What the service does

Letterhome prints the letter content you submit, places it in an envelope with the addresses you provide, applies Canadian postage, and deposits it with Canada Post for delivery. We are a printing and mailing intermediary — we are not a carrier or a courier.

  • Ensuring you have the right to send any documents you attach — do not attach documents containing third-party confidential information without authorisation.
