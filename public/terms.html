What the service does

Letterhome reviews each order, then has the letter content you submit printed in black & white by default (colour printing available for an additional fee) by the print and mail facility that produces your letter. It's folded into a window envelope addressed to the recipient you specify, metered Canadian postage is applied, and the envelope is handed to Canada Post as Lettermail. We are a printing and mailing intermediary. We are not a carrier or a courier.
