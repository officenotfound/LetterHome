A text disappears in a scroll. A letter sits on a counter, gets reread, gets kept. Here's how to send one to family or someone you love in Canada, from wherever you are.

Write a Letter →

You don't need a Canadian address, a printer, or a stamp. Write it, or attach a photo and a note as a PDF, and it's printed and mailed from inside Canada, arriving as a real envelope in a real mailbox.

How it works

  1. Write what you actually want to say

    Type it directly, or write it by hand and photograph or scan it as a PDF. Either works.

  2. Add a photo if you want

    Attach up to 5 files, a printed photo alongside a letter is a common way people use this for family back home.

  3. Send it

    Go to /send, enter the recipient's address, and we print and mail it from Canada within one business day.

Why it works for family who don't do email

Older relatives who don't check email or don't want to learn a new app still open physical mail. If someone back home reads letters better than screens, this reaches them the way they actually want to be reached.

Common questions

Can I send this without a return address?

Yes, if you'd rather keep it anonymous, we support sending without a return address.

Can I include a printed photo?

Yes, attach it as an image file alongside your letter, up to 5 attachments per order.

Does it need to be typed?

No, handwrite it and scan or photograph it as a PDF if you'd rather send it in your own handwriting.

How much does it cost?

$10 CAD to a Canadian address, $20 CAD anywhere else in the world.
