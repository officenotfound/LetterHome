Calculating the termination date

Under the Residential Tenancies Act, an N4 requires at least 14 days' notice for monthly tenants (7 days for weekly or daily tenancies). When you serve by mail, Ontario law adds 5 days to account for delivery.

Example: you place your order on July 22. We mail it July 23. Add 5 days for mail service = July 28 is the deemed service date. Add 14 days = the earliest valid termination date is August 11.

Write that date on your N4 form before uploading. If the date is too early, the LTB may dismiss your application.

What to use as the return address

You can use your Canadian property address as the return address, or skip the return address entirely — we offer that option on the order form. Landlords who are abroad often omit the return address to avoid confusion, since replies should go to an email or Canadian property manager.

Common questions

Can I serve an N4 notice by mail?

Yes. The Ontario Residential Tenancies Act allows landlords to serve an N4 by mail to the rental unit. When served by mail, you must add 5 days to the notice period to account for delivery. Canada Post lettermail is an accepted method.

Does the N4 need to be sent from a Canadian address?

No — it needs to be mailed from within Canada so it arrives with a Canadian postmark via Canada Post. Your return address can be your property address, a property manager's address, or omitted. Where you personally are located does not matter.

Can I email an N4 to my tenant instead?

Only if the tenant has agreed in writing to receive notices by email. Without that agreement, email is not a valid method of service under the RTA. Mail is the safest option if you don't have written consent on file.

What if my tenant ignores the N4?

If the tenant does not pay the overdue rent or move out by the termination date, you can file an L1 Application to Evict a Tenant for Non-payment of Rent with the LTB. You will need to show that the N4 was properly served — your Letterhome order confirmation provides proof of the mailing date.
