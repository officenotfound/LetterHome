What you get

One letter from Santa, printed in Canada on letter-size paper and mailed in an envelope with postage included. It is a real piece of mail that arrives in a real mailbox, addressed to your child by name.

The letter is a short, warm page that uses the details you give. We do not invent facts about your child, and we do not claim it comes from the North Pole post office. It is a letter you arrange for your child, from Santa.

When to order

For delivery before Christmas within Canada, order by December 5. That date is our estimate from typical Canada Post lettermail times (up to 2 weeks) plus a buffer for holiday volume. It is not a guarantee. Our holiday order-by dates explain how we work out the timing.

Prefer the free option? Canada Post runs a free program where children write to Santa and get a reply. Our guide to letters from Santa in Canada explains how it works and compares the two. You can also copy our free Santa letter template and mail it yourself.

Common questions

How much does a letter from Santa cost?

From $12 for a one-page letter to a Canadian address, with printing, envelope and postage included. Colour printing is $1 extra, and choosing the mailing date is free.

Can I see the letter before I pay?

Yes. The full letter appears on the page as you type, and the printed letter uses exactly that text.

Can you mail it outside Canada?

Not yet. Letters from Santa go to Canadian addresses only for now. A grandparent abroad can still order one for a grandchild in Canada.

When will it arrive?

We send it within two business days of the order, or on the date you choose. Canada Post lettermail takes up to 2 weeks within Canada and has no tracking, so we cannot promise an arrival date. Ordering by December 5 is our estimate for arriving before Christmas.

Is it from the North Pole post office?

No. It is printed in Canada and mailed by Canada Post like any other letter. It may not carry a special postmark.

What do you do with my child's details?

We use them to write and mail the letter, and you can ask us to delete them at any time at support@letterhome.ca. See our privacy policy.

What if it does not arrive?

Standard mail has no tracking, so we can't offer a reprint, resend or refund once a letter has been mailed. If it hasn't arrived after the estimated time, email us with your order number and we'll review it. Details are on our refunds page.
