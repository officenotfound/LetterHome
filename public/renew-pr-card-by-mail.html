Most people renew online, but the paper IMM 5444 route still exists and is sometimes the more reliable path if your online account has an issue or you're outside Canada without stable access.

How it works

  1. Prepare the IMM 5444 application

    Download the current kit and instructions from canada.ca. Include photos meeting the specification and any required supporting documents.

  2. Confirm the current mailing address

    IRCC mailing addresses for paper applications are listed on the same page and can change, verify it before sending rather than reusing an old address.

  3. Upload it at Letterhome

    Go to /send, enter the IRCC processing address as the recipient, and attach your completed package.

  4. We mail it the next business day

    Your application goes out via Canada Post with a valid Canadian mailing date and a confirmation email as your record.

Meet the residency requirement first: PR card renewal has its own physical presence requirement, separate from citizenship. Confirm you qualify before applying, IRCC's page covers the current rule.

Processing times

Paper applications generally take longer to process than online ones. Check current IRCC processing times before deciding which route fits your timeline.

Common questions

Can I renew my PR card from outside Canada?

Renewal generally requires meeting a physical presence requirement in Canada. Confirm your eligibility on IRCC's site before applying, this guide only covers how to mail the completed package.

Is paper slower than the online application?

Usually yes. Paper applications typically take longer to process. Use paper when the online portal isn't a workable option for you.

What photos do I need?

IRCC has a specific photo specification for PR card applications, listed on their page. Photos that don't meet spec are a common cause of returned applications.

How much does it cost to mail?

$10 CAD for a domestic Canadian address, covering printing, postage, and mailing. IRCC application fees are separate and paid directly to IRCC.
