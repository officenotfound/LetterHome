Letter writing

How to write a complaint
letter to a CEO

When customer service has gone nowhere, a short, factual letter to the top often gets a problem to the right person.

Send a letter →

Send a one-page letter to the CEO at the company's headquarters. Give your account or order number, what went wrong, what you have already tried, and exactly what you want done by a date. Many large companies pass these letters to an executive customer care team that can fix things normal support cannot.

Updated November 2026

Why writing to the CEO works

A CEO rarely reads every letter, but at many large companies, mail to the chief executive is passed to a team that handles escalated complaints. That team can often make decisions that front-line support cannot, such as refunds outside the normal rules. A clear, polite letter on paper is hard to ignore.

Find the headquarters address

  • The company's website. Look under Contact, About, Investors or Corporate information for a headquarters or corporate office address.
  • Public company filings. US public companies list the address of their principal executive offices on the cover of their annual report, Form 10-K, which you can find free on the SEC's EDGAR search.
  • The CEO's name. It is usually on the company's leadership or investor relations page. Address the letter to them by name.

What to put in the letter

  1. Your details: name, address, email, phone, and your account, order or case number.
  2. What happened, in short dated points. Stick to facts.
  3. What you have already tried: who you spoke to, when, and what they said.
  4. What you want: a refund, a repair, a replacement or a correction, and a reasonable date, such as 14 days.

Keep it to one page and stay polite. Anger makes a letter easier to dismiss. Do not send original receipts; send copies and keep the originals.

A template you can adapt

Dear Ms. Lee,

I am writing about order 48213, placed on September 3, 2026.

The laptop arrived with a cracked screen on September 8. I reported it
the same day (case 77120) and was promised a replacement twice, on
September 15 and October 2. No replacement has arrived.

I would like a full refund of $1,149.00 to my original card by
December 10, 2026. Copies of my receipt and case emails are enclosed.

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
Alex Morgan

If nothing happens

Keep a copy of your letter and the date you sent it. If the company does not respond, you can file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau for banking and credit problems, with the Better Business Bureau, or with your state attorney general's consumer protection office.

Common questions

Can you write a letter directly to a CEO?

Yes. Address it to the CEO by name at the company headquarters. At many large companies, an executive customer care team answers these letters.

How do I find a company CEO's address?

Use the headquarters address on the company's website, or for a US public company, the address of its principal executive offices on the cover of its Form 10-K on the SEC's EDGAR search.

What should I include in a complaint letter to a CEO?

Your account or order number, a short dated account of what happened, what you have already tried, and what you want done by a specific date.

Should I send a complaint letter by email or mail?

Either can work. A printed letter to the headquarters often reaches a different team than the support inbox, which is why people use it after support has failed.

What if the company ignores my letter?

Keep a copy and the date sent, then complain to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, the Better Business Bureau or your state attorney general.
