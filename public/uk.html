Addressing a letter

Within the UK, put each part on its own line and finish with the postcode.

Mrs A. Smith
12 High Street
Bath
BA1 1AA
UNITED KINGDOM

UK postcodes mix letters and numbers, such as SW1A 1AA or M1 1AE. Not sure how a Canadian address looks? See addressing a letter to Canada.

Common questions

Can I post a letter within the UK?

Yes. Where our local printing is running, the letter is printed and posted inside the UK. Otherwise it is printed in Canada and posted from there, which takes longer. The order page tells you which applies, with the price, before you pay.

Can I send letters from the UK to family in other countries?

Yes. We send letters to Canada, the US, Australia and most other countries. Pick your route above for the address format and price.

Is Letterhome part of a postal service?

No. Letterhome is an independent company. We print your letter and hand it to the mail service for delivery, and every address is verified before printing.

Do you charge in pounds?

Yes. The prices on this page are what you pay at checkout.

How long does it take?

We send it within two business days of the order. Delivery within Canada usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, and international delivery up to 4 weeks. Standard post has no tracking, so we cannot give an exact date.

Is it tracked or signed for?

No. Standard post has no tracking, signature or proof of delivery. You can follow your order through our own steps on the track page, and see our proof of mailing guide for the options.

Can I send it anonymously?

Yes. Skip the return address when you order. The envelope then carries our return address instead.
