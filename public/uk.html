Where our UK service is running, you also see a price for letters printed and posted inside the UK. The order page shows the exact price and delivery time for your address before you pay.

Each price covers one letter with printing, the envelope and postage. We price in Canadian dollars (CAD). The pound figures are estimates, refreshed weekly and rounded up to the nearest 50p. The exact amount you pay is shown at checkout, and your card issuer may convert it at its own rate. The first 2 black and white pages are included. Colour printing is C$1 extra, and each extra page is C$0.45 (C$0.55 in colour).

How it works

  1. Write it online

    Type your letter or attach a PDF or Word file, from anywhere in the UK. Photos work too, with colour printing.

  2. Add the address

    Enter the Canadian address, or any other. Add your own UK address as the return address, or skip it and send anonymously.

  3. We print and mail it from Canada

    A person checks every order, then it is printed, put in an envelope and mailed with Canada Post. You get an email when it is mailed.

  4. It arrives in a Canadian mailbox

    As a local letter, with no Canadian address or stamp needed from you.

Why people in the UK use it

Plenty of people in the UK still have paperwork that has to reach a Canadian address on paper:

  • Tax and benefits. CRA forms and non-resident tax returns, CPP and OAS applications.
  • Immigration. IRCC documents for people applying to live, work or study in Canada.
  • Students. Transcripts and application documents for Canadian universities.
  • Family and friends. A real letter for someone in Canada, timed with schedule for later.

Common questions

Can I send a letter to Canada from the UK?

Yes. Write it on our site, and we print it and mail it from inside Canada, so it arrives as a local letter. You do not need a printer, stamps or a Canadian address.

Do you charge in pounds?

We price in Canadian dollars. The pound figures on this page are estimates, refreshed weekly and rounded up to the nearest 50p. The exact amount you pay is shown at checkout, and your card issuer may convert it at its own rate.

How long does it take?

We send it within two business days of the order. Delivery within Canada typically takes up to 2 weeks, and international delivery up to 4 weeks. Lettermail has no tracking, so we cannot give an exact date.

Can I post a letter within the UK?

Where our UK service is running, yes, and the price shows above and on the order page. Otherwise the letter is printed and mailed from Canada, which takes longer. The order page tells you which applies, with the price, before you pay.

Can I send official documents?

Yes. We print and mail what you upload. We do not check forms for you, so make sure the document is complete and signed where needed. See our list of forms that need mail.

Is it recorded or signed for?

No. Standard lettermail has no tracking, signature or proof of delivery. See our proof of mailing guide for the options.

Can I post anonymously?

Yes. Skip the return address when you order. The envelope then carries our return address instead.
