How it works

  1. Write it online

    Type your letter or attach a PDF or Word file. Photos work too, with colour printing.

  2. Add the address

    Anywhere in the UK, Canada, the US, the UK, Australia or New Zealand, or anywhere else. Add your own return address, or skip it and send anonymously.

  3. We print it and post it

    A person checks every order, then it's printed, put in an envelope and posted. You'll get an email when it's on its way.

  4. It arrives in their letterbox

    No printer, no stamps and no queue at the post office.

Addressing a letter

Within the UK, put each part on its own line and finish with the postcode.

Mrs A. Smith
12 High Street
Bath
BA1 1AA
UNITED KINGDOM

UK postcodes mix letters and numbers, such as SW1A 1AA or M1 1AE. Not sure how a Canadian address looks? See addressing a letter to Canada.

Forms and paperwork

Some people also use us for forms that have to reach Canada on paper: CRA tax forms, non-resident tax returns, IRCC documents and student transcripts. We print and post what you upload and don't check forms for you, so make sure they're complete and signed where needed.

Standard post has no tracking and may not carry a dated postmark, so it is not proof of posting. See our proof of mailing guide if you need proof.

Common questions

Can I post a letter within the UK?

Yes. Where our local printing is running, the letter is printed and posted inside the UK. Otherwise it is printed in Canada and posted from there, which takes longer. The order page tells you which applies, with the price, before you pay.

Can I send letters from the UK to family in other countries?

Yes. We send letters to Canada, the US, the UK, Australia, New Zealand and most other countries. Pick your route above for the address format and price.

Do you charge in pounds?

We price in Canadian dollars. The pound figures on this page are estimates, refreshed weekly and rounded up to the nearest 50p. The exact amount shows at checkout, and your bank may convert it at its own rate.

How long does it take?

We send it within two business days of the order. Delivery within Canada typically takes up to 2 weeks, and international delivery up to 4 weeks. Standard post has no tracking, so we can't give an exact date.

Is it tracked or signed for?

No. Standard post has no tracking, signature or proof of delivery. See our proof of mailing guide for the options.

Can I send it anonymously?

Yes. Skip the return address when you order. The envelope then carries our return address instead.
