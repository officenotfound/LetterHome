IRCC mailing addresses, by application type

IRCC doesn't use one mailing address, it uses dozens, sorted by application type and processing centre. This page is a map of the ones we've verified directly against IRCC's own contact pages, with links to a full guide for each. Always confirm against the instructions in your own application package before mailing anything important.

Updated October 2026

Most of IRCC's physical mail, regardless of which office technically processes your file, ends up at one of a small number of PO Boxes in Sydney and New Waterford, Nova Scotia, where it's scanned into IRCC's digital case system. Which box you use is what tells IRCC's intake process which queue your mail belongs in.

Citizenship

Citizenship grants and resumptions go to CPC-Sydney Box 7000 (Canadian Armed Forces applicants use Box 8200). Proof of citizenship, search requests, and specifically requested documents use three further boxes at the same centre, 10000, 10010, and 12,000 respectively.

Permanent residence

PR card renewals and replacements are mailed to Box 10020 (or Box 9000 for correspondence, depending on your application guide's instructions) in Sydney. Sponsorship correspondence and right of permanent residence fee paperwork tied to CPC-Mississauga routes through the IRCC Digitization Centre's PO Box 12153.

Everything else

Study permits, work permits, visitor visas, and many other application types each have their own current mailing instructions published in the specific application guide on canada.ca. If you don't see your situation above, check your application's own instructions, or see our general guide to mailing IRCC documents from outside Canada for the broader process.
