Who it is for

People who would love to get real mail, and the people who miss them.

A grandparent in a care home

A retirement or long-term care home often has a mail table or a name on the door. A letter on the same day each month gives them something to look forward to.

A relative in hospital or recovery

For a long stay, a letter or postcard a month from home. Check the hospital's mail rules first, and we will help you address it.

A veteran or someone who served

Letters and photos to a veteran at home, in a care facility or in a veterans' residence, on a day you pick.

Someone serving or deployed

Where the address and the country allow mail, a letter from home on a schedule. Delivery can be slow, so we plan around it.

Family living abroad

A parent, sibling or friend in another country. Letters go to over 160 countries, delivered typically up to four weeks.

Someone who travels a lot

Hard to reach by phone and always moving? A monthly letter to a home address waits for them.

Run a care home, a hospital programme or a veterans' group and want to send to many people? Ask about group mailing.

How it would work

  1. Choose who it goes toAdd the recipient's address once, in Canada or abroad.
  2. Pick a day each monthPick a day each month, say the 1st, or the Sunday nearest the 15th.
  3. Get a reminder, then writeWe email you a reminder a few days before, with a prompt if you are stuck. Write online or attach a PDF with photos.
  4. We print and mail itWe verify the address, print and mail it. If you skip a month, nothing is mailed.
