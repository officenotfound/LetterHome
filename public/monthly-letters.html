Who it is for

People who would love to get real mail, and the people who miss them:

  • A grandparent in a retirement or long-term care home.
  • A parent, sibling or friend who lives in another country.
  • Someone who travels a lot and is hard to reach by phone.
  • Anyone you mean to write to and never get around to.

How it would work

  1. Choose who it goes to

    Add the recipient's address once, in Canada or abroad.

  2. Pick a day each month

    Say the 1st, or the Sunday nearest the 15th. You choose.

  3. Get a reminder, then write

    We email you a few days before, with a prompt if you are stuck. Write online or attach a PDF with photos.

  4. We print and mail it

    Every address is verified before printing. If you skip a month, nothing is mailed.

Common questions

Can I sign up for monthly letters today?

Not yet. This page collects interest so we can build it around how people actually want to use it. You can send single letters now from $6.

How much will it cost?

We have not set the price. We will announce it to the list when it opens. A single letter is $6 to Canada or the United States today.

Will I get other emails if I join?

No. You get one email when monthly letters open. You can ask us to remove you at any time at support@letterhome.ca.

Can it go to a retirement home?

Yes. See our guide to mailing a retirement or nursing home for how to address it.

Can I send letters to another country?

Letters go to over 160 countries today. International delivery is typically up to 4 weeks, so a monthly letter would be planned around that.
