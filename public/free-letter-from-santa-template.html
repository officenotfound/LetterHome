Make it feel personal

  • Be specific. "I heard you learned to ride your bike without training wheels" beats "you have been good".
  • Keep it short. One page is plenty, and a child can read it in one go.
  • Use their words. If they call their blanket "Bunny", Santa knows it as Bunny.
  • Change the handwriting. If you print it, write the signature in a different pen from your usual one.
  • Ask for one small thing. A goal like "help with the dishes" or "keep practising your reading" works better than a lecture.
