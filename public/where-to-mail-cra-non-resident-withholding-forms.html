Where to mail NR73, NR74 & non-resident withholding forms to the CRA

Residency determination requests (NR73, NR74) and non-resident withholding correspondence both go to the CRA's Non-Resident Withholding Section in Sudbury, a different Sudbury address than the one used for T1 returns. If you're being told to confirm your residency status for tax purposes, or you handle NR4 withholding for a non-resident payee, this is the address the CRA itself publishes.

Updated October 2026

Two common situations send paper to this office: filing Form NR73 (Determination of Residency Status – Leaving Canada) or Form NR74 (Determination of Residency Status – Entering Canada) to get the CRA's opinion on your residency status, and general non-resident withholding correspondence tied to NR4 slips, NR601/NR602 waivers, and related Part XIII withholding matters.

Always check the form itself: NR73 and NR74 both print their current mailing instructions directly on the form. If the address on your copy of the form differs from the one above, follow the form, it's the CRA's most current source for that specific submission.
