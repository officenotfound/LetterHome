Where to mail documents to WES Canada

Documents for a World Education Services (WES) Canada credential evaluation go by regular post to WES Global Documentation Centre, PO Box 2008 STN Main, Newmarket, ON, L3Y 0G5, Canada. Courier shipments use a separate street address in Aurora, Ontario. Both are published on WES's own Canadian help pages.

Updated October 2026

If you're a newcomer or immigration applicant outside Canada who needs an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) from WES, and your institution can only issue paper transcripts or certificates, you'll need to get those sealed documents into the mail addressed to WES's Newmarket processing centre.

Courier address is different: for express courier (not regular post), WES publishes a separate street address — 14–145 Industrial Pkwy South, Aurora, ON, L4G 3V5, Canada. Documents cannot be hand-delivered to WES in person.

Electronic submission may be available: some institutions have a Digital Partnership arrangement with WES to send transcripts electronically. Check with your issuing institution before defaulting to mail.
